**Word count:** ~3,600 words

**Key Pakistan differentiators:**
1. Transmitter vs switch confusion: the most common Pakistan project specification error — switch gives YES/NO, transmitter gives continuous 4-20mA
2. WAPDA sag kills loop supplies: 220V ±10% SMPS cuts out at 198VAC; Pakistan sags to 170–180VAC → specify 85–264VAC wide-range SMPS
3. VFD noise destroys raw TC/RTD signals: head-mounted transmitter converts to 4-20mA at sensor → immune to conducted/radiated VFD noise in cable trays
4. Thermocouple copper extension cable: creates spurious second junction → 5–15°C systematic offset — fundamental Pakistan site error
5. HART 250Ω rule: PLC AI cards at 100–150Ω + external 100Ω resistor needed; missed on ~70% of Pakistan HART installations
6. DP transmitter for filter differential: Pakistan plants use two switches; DP transmitter gives continuous clog monitoring with trending
7. WAPDA transient spikes: 2–5kV switching transients destroy transmitter electronics → specify TVS diode or loop surge arrester
8. PT100 3-wire vs 2-wire: cable resistance error = ~2.6°C/Ω for PT100 — 2-wire should never be used on runs > 5m
