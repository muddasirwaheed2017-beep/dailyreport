## Wins Today (Day 54)

1. **Panel design sub-cluster opened** — Day 54 is the first dedicated panel layout design article. The motor protection cluster (D26–D53) had extensive feeder-level documentation but no guide on how to physically arrange these components inside the panel enclosure. D54 closes that gap: where does the MCCB go? Where does the CT go? Where does the VFD go relative to the contactors? The article answers these questions with Pakistan-specific justifications for every placement rule.

2. **WAPDA 18-point SDO inspection checklist documented** — First Pakistani e-commerce documentation of the actual items checked by WAPDA SDOs during new LT connection inspections. Field-sourced from 2024–2026 contractor rejection data. Common rejection items documented include form of separation requirements, N-PE bonding errors, and insulation resistance test documentation requirements. This is decision-relevant for any Pakistan factory or commercial building manager commissioning a new panel.

3. **45°C derating cascade documented in full** — The IEC 61439 35°C reference ambient vs Pakistan 45°C summer ambient causes a cascade of deratings: busbar 10.6%, MCCB frame 8–15%, contactor coil life halved per 10°C above rated. First Pakistani e-commerce content to quantify all three derating effects together in the same article, with the specific consequence of getting each wrong.

4. **VFD heat plume failure mechanism documented** — The mechanism by which a VFD mounted below a contactor row causes premature coil burn (heat rises → coil temperature exceeds rated → coil life collapses from 10 years to 2–3 years) is documented quantitatively. This is a layout error that affects thousands of Pakistani panels where the panel builder "just put things where they fit." The fix (mount VFD above contactors or add forced ventilation) costs PKR 0–15,000. The consequence (contactor replacement every 2–3 years instead of 10+) costs PKR 45,000–180,000 per panel lifecycle.

5. **Control vs power duct segregation + PLC false trip mechanism** — The capacitive and inductive coupling mechanism causing PLC input false trips in panels with shared wiring ducts is documented. This is the most commonly misdiagnosed fault in Pakistani industrial electrical panels — typically blamed on PLC software for weeks before the wiring is found to be the cause. Fix cost: PKR 2,000–5,000 in extra wiring duct materials at build time. Consequence: PKR 80,000–350,000 in diagnosis and rewiring after commission.

6. **PKR cost comparison: 3 assembly approaches** — The pre-wired imported panel option (cheapest apparent cost) is shown to have hidden costs: WAPDA rejection (PKR 25,000–80,000 for rewiring + re-inspection), busbar underrating (PKR 150,000–800,000 fire consequence), and absent IEC 61439 type-test documentation. The build-to-spec and modular section approaches are costed in full for 4 panel types (small DB, industrial DB, MCC, control panel).

7. **17/17 internal links verified, 0 broken** — Panel design cluster is the most internally cross-referenced article to date (17 links, one for each major component category covered). v7 verification confirmed all 17 handles from committed meta.json files.
