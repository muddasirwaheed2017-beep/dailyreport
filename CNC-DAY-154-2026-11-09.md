## Step 3 — Today's Topic

**Day 154:** Power Quality Disturbance Recorder Pakistan 2026
- Cluster: **Industrial Power Quality & Energy Management D2/3**
- Previous: D153 EMS & ISO 50001 (D1/3 ✅)
- Next: D155 Automatic Demand Response & Load Shedding Controller (D3/3)

**Topic rationale:** Pakistan's grid delivers 20–80 voltage sag events per week to 11 kV industrial feeders in major industrial zones (Faisalabad FESCO, Lahore LESCO, Karachi KESC). Without a Class A IEC 61000-4-30 recorder, factories have zero evidentiary basis for WAPDA/DISCO dispute claims. NEPRA Power Quality Regulations 2022 specifically require Class A measurement for formal PCC complaints. This is the highest-ROI electrical product category for Pakistani factories consuming >500 kVA — one successful WAPDA dispute claim (PKR 1–3M equipment damage) covers the recorder cost with margin. No deep-technical Pakistani buyer's guide for PQDRs exists; this article fills a unique content gap, targeting engineers and procurement managers who already understand they have a PQ problem but need guidance on the measurement+legal framework.
