**Topic rationale:** Completes the 3-day Power Measurement & Energy Management cluster:
- D104: IEC 61000-4-30 Power Quality Measurement Methodology (how to measure the problem)
- D105: Industrial Multi-Function Energy Meter Pakistan 2026 (continuous monitoring instrument)
- D106: APFC Panel Pakistan 2026 (reactive power correction — solving the problem)

The cluster follows a logical measurement→monitoring→correction workflow. D106 is the action step: after measuring PQ (D104) and monitoring kVAR demand (D105), D106 covers how to deploy reactive power compensation that eliminates the NEPRA PF penalty and reduces kVA demand billing.

**Pakistan-specific differentiators (7 unique):**
1. NEPRA PF penalty threshold 0.85 lagging — stricter than India (0.90), Gulf states (0.92)
2. WAPDA/DISCO TOU billing window mismatch (18:00–22:00 peak) — APFC response time must be <2 sec to capture reactive corrections during peak window
3. VFD-heavy textile/cement plants (Faisalabad, Karachi) causing 5th/7th harmonic resonance with standard capacitor banks — detuned p=7% reactors mandatory where VFD >20% of load
4. Generator island-mode instability: captive generation during loadshedding causes voltage overshoot (110–115% Vn) when APFC over-compensates on "soft" generator bus
5. Pakistani summer ambient 45–50°C requiring capacitor KVAR derating of 8–17% above 40°C rated value
6. DISCO billing period alignment: 30-day fixed billing means low-load periods (Ramazan single shift, weekend skeleton staff) risk over-correction and leading PF charges
7. Mixed tariff consumers (D2 category, some B2) where reactive penalty calculation methodology differs between DISCO rate schedules — target PF 0.92–0.95 provides safe margin for all categories
