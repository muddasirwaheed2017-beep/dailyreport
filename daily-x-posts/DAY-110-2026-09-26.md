## Post

🔌 63% of Pakistani industrial DG sets are UNDERSIZED or wrongly rated.

The most expensive mistake: ordering a "500kVA" standby generator for a HESCO zone running 9h/day load-shedding.

Standby (ESP) rating = 500 hours/year max.
HESCO 9h/day × 300 days = 2,700 hours/year.

You'll burn out the engine in under 2 months.

Rule: HESCO/QESCO zones → always specify **Prime (PRP) rating** and run at 80% max load.

Also — Chitral buyers: your 500kVA generator delivers only 455kVA at 1,500m altitude. Order 630kVA.

Plus NEPRA now fines PKR 500,000 per incident for unregistered DGs >100kVA.
