## Post 1 — Education Hook (Pakistan problem framing)
Your WAPDA UPS dies after 12 months.

Blame the weather, not the battery.

At 45°C in an unventilated electrical room, a genuine VRLA battery lasts 12–18 months.

At 25°C? Same battery → 3–5 years.

AC in the UPS room isn't a luxury. It's a 3× battery life multiplier.

## Post 2 — Generator Compatibility Pain Point
"My UPS goes to battery every time the generator starts."

Most likely cause: generator THDv 8–12%, UPS accepts max 5%.

Fix: Switch UPS to Generator Mode (widens tolerance) OR add isolation transformer between generator and UPS.

Don't keep burning battery cycles. Fix the root cause.

## Post 3 — Topology Education (Thread)
Thread: Offline vs Line-Interactive vs Online UPS — which Pakistan site needs which?

Offline UPS (8–20 ms transfer):
→ Single PC, router ✅
→ Server with active PFC supply ❌
→ Medical equipment ❌
→ PLC / VFD ❌

Line-Interactive (4–8 ms, built-in AVR):
→ Office 10–30 PCs ✅
→ WAPDA voltage swings absorbed on AVR ✅
→ Server room ❌ (needs 0 ms)
→ Medical ❌ (needs 0 ms)

Online Double-Conversion (0 ms):
→ Server room ✅
→ Medical/ICU ✅
→ Industrial automation ✅
→ Data centre ✅

Pakistan rule: If in doubt, go Online.

## Post 5 — Solar Hybrid Tip
Solar hybrid UPS ≠ solar inverter.

Solar inverter (grid-tie only): shuts off during outage. Anti-islanding.

Solar hybrid UPS: keeps your load running from battery + solar during outage. MPPT built-in.

For Pakistan: you need the hybrid. Generator-only sites can skip panels for now.

AEDB net metering bonus: hybrid UPS can export to grid AND provide backup. Check NEPRA approval list first.
