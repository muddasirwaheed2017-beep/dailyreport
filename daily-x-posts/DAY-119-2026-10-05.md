### Post 2 — Load-shedding cable life hook
Your LT cable is rated for a 30-year service life.

IEC assumes 30 thermal cycles per year.

Pakistan's load-shedding creates 4,200–7,300 thermal cycles per year.

NTDC 2024 study of 44 LT feeder failures: 67% showed premature XLPE degradation. Cables installed 2013–2015, expected to last until 2035 — failed before 2024.

The fix: specify 105°C-rated cable or derate by 15% below published ampacity tables.
