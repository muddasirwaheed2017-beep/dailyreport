**Tweet 2 / The WAPDA asymmetry problem**
Pakistan WAPDA industrial feeders regularly show 2–5% voltage unbalance in summer.

IEC 60034-26 says 4% voltage unbalance = 32% MORE rotor heating.

Your thermal OL relay? It measures current — it does NOT see this.
Your phase failure relay? It requires near-COMPLETE phase loss to trip — not 4% asymmetry.

Only negative-sequence protection (built into digital relays) catches this before your motor dies.

**Tweet 4 / Pakistan load-shedding and thermal memory**

**Tweet 6 / Trip Class — Pakistan Selection**
Which trip class for Pakistan loads?

Class 10 → centrifugal pumps, fans, tube-well pumps (up to 10s at 7.2× FLC)
Class 20 → compressors, loaded belt conveyors
Class 30 → crushing mills, sugar rollers, rubber mixers

Most Pakistan panel builders set Class 10 for everything. This causes:
- Nuisance trips on heavy-load starting
- Or (worse) setting OL too high to "stop the tripping" — removing the protection

Match class to your load. Takes 30 seconds to set.

**Tweet 7 / Wiring mistake Pakistan panel builders make**
Protection relay trip contact wired as NORMALLY OPEN (NO)?

That's wrong. Here's why:

If the relay's control supply fails → NO contact stays open → motor keeps running UNPROTECTED.

Always wire trip contacts NORMALLY CLOSED (NC) in series with the contactor coil.

If control supply fails → NC opens → contactor drops → motor stops. SAFE.

Pakistan MCC builders: check your panel drawings. This mistake is extremely common.
