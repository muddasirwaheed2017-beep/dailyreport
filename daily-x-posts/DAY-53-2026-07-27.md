Pakistan WAPDA supply quality problems a basic voltmeter misses:

- Voltage unbalance % (negative-sequence, per IEC 61000-4-30)
- Individual harmonic orders (5th = VFD, 3rd = single-phase loads)
- Displacement PF vs distortion PF (different corrections)
- Voltage dips from feeder switching
- Flicker from arc furnaces / welders on shared LT network
