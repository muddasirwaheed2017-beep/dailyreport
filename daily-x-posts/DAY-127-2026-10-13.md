**Reply/thread (technical hook):**

Pakistan engineers: ambient derating for LT cable is one of the most missed items.

At 50°C ambient (Karachi/Multan summer), a 70°C PVC cable is derated to ~71% of its rated current.

That 35mm² cable you sized for 100A? It now carries only ~83A.

One size up saves expensive rework.
