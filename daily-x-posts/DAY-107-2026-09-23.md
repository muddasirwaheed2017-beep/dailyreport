### Tweet 3 (IEEE 519 Quick Reference)
IEEE 519-2022 PCC limits for Pakistan:

415 V (LV): THDv ≤ 8%, individual ≤ 5%
11 kV (most industrial): THDv ≤ 5%, individual ≤ 3%
66/132 kV (WAPDA grid): THDv ≤ 2.5%

Your 6-pulse VFD with no reactor typically injects 25–35% THDi.
The allowed limit for most sites: 5–8%.

---

### Tweet 4 (Fix)
Mitigation in order of cost/effectiveness:

1. 3% line reactor on each 6-pulse VFD — PKR 8k–110k, cuts 5th harmonic 40–50%
2. Detuned APFC (p=7%) — PF correction without resonance
3. Active harmonic filter — PKR 650k–3.8M, 95–98% cancellation, dynamic response

Start with reactors. Measure again. Then size AHF to what's left.
