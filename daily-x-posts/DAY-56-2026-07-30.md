### Thread Post 2

**The 40% fill rule — why Pakistan needs 35%**

EN 50085 says max 40% of duct area can be cables.

But that 40% assumes 30°C ambient.

Karachi, Multan, Lahore in July = 45°C ambient.

At 45°C, conductor temp already up 15°C before you add load.

Pakistani rule: target 35% fill. The 5% is your summer thermal margin.

### Thread Post 3

**Slotted vs solid duct — one line answer**

Slotted (grey): control wiring. Cables exit through slots to reach each component.

Solid (black): power cables. Cables only exit at duct ends.

Most Pakistani MCC panels: both. Grey slotted for 1.5mm² control. Black solid for 6mm²+ power runs.

### Thread Post 4

**Mini duct — why PLC panels need a separate duct for signal cables**

A 4–20 mA loop signal = 16 mA full scale.

Route that signal cable next to a 1.5mm² contactor coil cable?

Inductive coupling injects 0.5–2 mA noise.

That's 3–12% measurement error on your pressure transmitter.

Mini duct (25×16mm, PKR 120–200/2m) prevents this.

### Thread Post 5

**VFD panel wiring duct separation rule**

VFD output motor cables: separate solid duct, min 200mm from any signal cable duct.

VFD control signal cables: separate mini duct, 90° crossing if paths must cross.

Mixing VFD power and signal cables in the same duct = spurious trips + 4–20mA signal corruption.

Correction after commissioning: PKR 25,000–120,000.

### Thread Post 6

**WAPDA SDO 5-point duct inspection checklist**

1. Fill ratio — cover closes without forcing
2. Cover fully seated and latched
3. Power and control cables separated
4. No sharp bends at slot exit points
5. No gaps between duct sections

Fail any one = rework + re-inspection.
Cost: PKR 8,000–60,000
