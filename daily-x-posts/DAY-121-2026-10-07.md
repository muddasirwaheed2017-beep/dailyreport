## Post 1 — Pakistan Soil Derating Hook

Your 11kV XLPE cable datasheet says 390A for 185mm² copper.

In Interior Sindh (dry sandy soil), the real number is ~300A.

That 23% gap isn't a cable defect — it's the difference between Pakistan's soil thermal resistivity (2.0–2.5 K·m/W) and the IEC standard assumption (1.0 K·m/W).

Size every MV cable for Pakistan conditions, not the datasheet.

## Post 2 — XLPE vs PILC Transition Joint Hook

Pakistan still has 500+ km of old PILC 11kV cable in LESCO/K-Electric urban networks.

When it fails: most contractors replace it with XLPE using a standard straight joint kit.

That's wrong — and it's the #1 cause of 11kV joint failures in Pakistan network rehabilitation.

XLPE-to-PILC transitions need a specific transition joint kit with oil stop barrier. Not a standard kit.

## Post 3 — DISCO Fault Level Table Hook

In Pakistan, the same 11kV cable cross-section is SAFE in MEPCO (16kA) and would BURN at LESCO Zone 1 (50kA).

120mm² copper survives 1s fault at 16kA.
At 50kA it would need 300mm².

Before specifying your 11kV cable:
— Confirm Isc at the nearest upstream substation from your DISCO
— Size conductor for fault withstand first, then check ampacity
