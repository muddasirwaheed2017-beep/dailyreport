## Thread 1

Most common servo fault in Pakistani industrial panels:

🌡️ Overtemperature

Servo drives in non-AC-cooled panels hit 55°C in Punjab summer.
Standard rating is 40°C ambient.

Fix: forced cooling or derate by 2–3% per °C above 40°C.
The drive that looks fine in October will trip in June.

## Thread 3

Pakistani machine shops: the shielded cable problem.

Servo encoder cable shield must earth at ONE end only (drive side).
Both ends = shield current loop = position noise = erratic cuts.

Also: never run servo cables in the same conduit as 415V power.
10 minutes of routing discipline saves days of troubleshooting.

Rs 450–950/m for proper double-shielded encoder cable. Budget for it.
