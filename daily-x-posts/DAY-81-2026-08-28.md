## Main Post
Your distribution transformer is installed. Now what?

The Main Distribution Board (MDB) decides everything from here:

⚡ How much 415V power gets where
🔒 How fast faults are isolated
🌡️ Whether busbars overheat at 45°C Pakistani summer
📊 Whether WAPDA can meter your consumption accurately

MDB sizing mistakes Pakistani engineers make:

❌ Sizing busbar for transformer kVA, not 125% future headroom
❌ Skipping 45°C derating (a 1,600A MDB only carries 1,408A at 45°C)
❌ Form 2 panel where WAPDA requires Form 3b
❌ Wrong CT class (0.5 vs 0.5S) causing billing disputes

DISCO fault levels matter:
• LESCO / K-Electric: 50kA → need 50kA-rated ACB
• IESCO / FESCO: 36kA
• MEPCO / PESCO: 25kA
