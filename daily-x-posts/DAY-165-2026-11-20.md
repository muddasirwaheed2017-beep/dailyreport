**Thread 1/5:**
Level 2 (7kW) = overnight home charge. 8–9 hours for 60kWh.
Level 3 DC fast (60kW) = 45 minutes. PKR 2.2M–3.5M hardware alone.

For 99% of Pakistanis: 7kW wallbox at home is the right answer.

---

**Thread 2/5:**
NTRC EV Policy 2023: Pakistan mandates Type 2 connector (IEC 62196-2) for all AC chargers.

Chinese EVs before mid-2024 may have GB/T connector (incompatible with Pakistani public chargers).

Always check your car's connector standard BEFORE buying a charger.

**Thread 3/5:**
LESCO off-peak EV tariff: PKR 24.5/kWh (11pm–7am).

Fill a 60kWh battery overnight = PKR 1,470.

Same 60kWh in petrol car at PKR 350/litre, 12 km/litre = PKR 1,750 for just 210 km.

The math is now clear.
