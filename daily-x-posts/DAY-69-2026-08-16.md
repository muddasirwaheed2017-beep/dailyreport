One more ATS setting most installers get wrong:

**Retransfer delay: set 30 sec ❌ → set 90 sec ✅**

Why? WAPDA in Lahore, Multan, Faisalabad often trips AGAIN within 20–40 seconds of restoring.

At 30s delay: load transfers back → generator cools → WAPDA trips → cold start again.
At 90s delay: ATS waits, WAPDA trips, generator keeps running. No interruption.

One setting change. Saves fuel, saves wear, saves a cold start.
