## Thread 3 — Generator Protection Angle

🚨 WAPDA load shedding just ended and your generator tripped on overload. Again.

Root cause: ATS transferred full load onto a genset sized for 60% of grid demand.

ADR fix:
→ Monitor ATS pre-transfer signal
→ Pre-shed non-critical loads in <5 seconds before ATS transfers
→ Genset starts at 60-70% load, ramps up smoothly
→ No trip. No stoppage. No damage.

One ADR controller. Two jobs: demand saving + genset protection.
