### Thread 1 — Pakistan Summer Derating

45°C ambient in Punjab summer ruins ball screws faster than anything else.

Root cause: standard EP2 grease melts to water-thin oil above 70°C, then oxidises into gritty varnish that scores ball races.

Fix: Mobilux EP 2 or SKF LGEP 2 — both rated stable to 130°C.
Regreasing interval in dusty mills: every 250–500 km travel (not annual).

### Thread 2 — Load Shedding DOL Restart Factor

When calculating linear actuator force in Pakistan, add a 2.5× multiplier on the acceleration component.

Why: DOL restart after load shedding creates 4–6× rated torque in the first 1–3 seconds. Ball screws at the end of their stroke can be preloaded to plastic deformation if the motor-to-screw coupling isn't the weakest link.

Size your MCB at 2× rated actuator current to survive DOL restart without nuisance trip.

### Thread 4 — Counterfeit Ball Screw Test

No lab needed. Field test in 30 seconds:

1. Weigh it. Genuine Ø20×1000mm SFU2005 = 850–920g. Fake = 700–780g (hollow shaft).
2. Wipe marking with acetone. Genuine = laser-etched, survives. Fake = smears off.
3. Mount nut, push axially with hand — feel for clunk. Genuine preload: 0.004–0.02mm. Fake: 0.05–0.2mm, perceptible clunk.

Pakistan market has significant counterfeit THK/Hiwin stock. Test before machining your bearing housings.
