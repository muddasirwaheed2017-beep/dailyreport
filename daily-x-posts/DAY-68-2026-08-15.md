## Tweet 2/4

Black cotton soil (Vertisol) covers 4.2 million hectares of lower Punjab + upper Sindh.

In monsoon it swells 15–30% volumetrically.

In dry season it contracts.

Result: your earth rod migrates UPWARD 8–14cm per monsoon cycle.

After 3 seasons? The rod connection emerges from the ground.

Anti-migration clamp at 300mm depth. Full bentonite fill. Non-negotiable in Multan/Bahawalpur/RYK.

## Tweet 3/4

Karachi DHA Phase 8, Bahria Town Karachi, PORT Qasim adjacent sites:

Soil pH 3.8–4.6.

Standard copper-bonded earth rods: 18–24 month lifespan in this pH range.

The copper coating de-bonds. The carbon steel core corrodes. Underground. No visible indicator.

Protocol: 316L solid stainless steel rod + magnesium sacrificial anode + bentonite buffer to pH 6.5–7.0.
