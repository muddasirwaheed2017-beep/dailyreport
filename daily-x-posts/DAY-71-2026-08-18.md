## Post 1 (Primary — Educational Hook)

🔌 Pakistan problem: Your WAPDA feeder drops to 158V in summer.

Most stabilizers only go down to 176V. That 18V gap = your AC compressor running unprotected.

The fix: specify 150V–270V input range, not ±20%.

## Post 2 (Sizing Rule)

📐 Pakistani AC sizing rule no one tells you:

❌ 1.5-ton AC = 1.5 KVA stabilizer
✅ 1.5-ton AC = 4 KVA stabilizer

Reason: compressor starting inrush hits 20–26A for 1.5 seconds.
Your 2 KVA unit can't handle it.

Rule: 1 ton AC → 3 KVA stabilizer
1.5 ton → 4 KVA
2 ton → 5 KVA

## Post 3 (Solar Integration Warning)

⚠️ Never put a servo stabilizer on your solar inverter OUTPUT.

The stabilizer fights the inverter's own voltage regulation = resonance = inverter shutdown.

Where to install it: WAPDA INPUT to the inverter.
Inverter output is already stable.
