### Thread Post 1

Pakistan compressed air quality problem (nobody talks about this):

1. Reciprocating piston compressors = 5× more oil carryover than rotary screw
2. Aging galvanized pipe = rust scale + abrasive particles
3. 30°C dewpoints + uninsulated pipes = condensate in every cylinder

An FRL unit is not optional in Pakistan. It's life support for your pneumatic system.

---

### Thread Post 2

The no-lube cylinder + lubricator trap:

Modern AirTAC and SMC cylinders have pre-greased "no-lube" seals.

Installing a lubricator downstream?

The oil mist WASHES OUT the factory grease.

Result: seals run dry → fail in weeks instead of years.

Check the cylinder data sheet BEFORE connecting an FRL lubricator.

### Thread Post 3

Pakistan load shedding and your pneumatic press clamp:

When power cuts, solenoid valve de-energizes → cylinder vents → clamp releases → workpiece falls.

Fix: install a pilot-operated check valve (POCV) between solenoid and cylinder.

Traps air in cylinder on power loss.

PKR 2,500–5,500 saves thousands in damaged parts and lost production.
