### Thread 1 — WAPDA Loop Supply Hazard
🚨 Pakistan 4-20mA loop power supply trap:

SMPS rated "220V ±10%" cuts out at 198VAC.
Pakistan voltage sags reach 170–180VAC during load-shedding peaks.

Your entire instrument loop goes dark. Every AI reads 0mA. Spurious trips.

**Fix:** Specify wide-range SMPS: 85–264VAC or 100–240VAC input.
Cost difference: ~PKR 500 per supply. Downtime cost: PKR 500,000+.

### Thread 2 — PT100 vs Thermocouple in One Rule
Pakistan temperature sensor selection in one rule:

- < 500°C + accuracy matters → PT100 RTD (±0.3°C achievable)
- > 500°C + high temperature range → Type K thermocouple (to 1,260°C)
- Cold store / pharma cold chain → Type T thermocouple (−200°C to +350°C)
- Long-term furnace stability → Type N (less drift than K above 800°C)

And NEVER extend a thermocouple with copper wire. Use matching extension wire or you'll have a spurious 5–15°C offset. Classic Pakistan site error.

### Thread 3 — Head-Mounted Transmitter VFD Fix
If your Pakistan plant runs VFDs everywhere and your temperature readings show ±5–15°C noise:

The raw PT100/thermocouple cable is picking up VFD switching noise in the cable tray.

Fix: Head-mounted transmitter at the sensor → outputs 4-20mA → screened pair back to panel.

4-20mA is current loop — immune to voltage noise pickup.
Noisy millivolt TC signal over 30m cable tray = rubbish.

Cost of head-mounted TT: PKR 4,500–9,500. Cost of wrong temperature data: production loss.

### Thread 4 — HART 250Ω Rule Pakistan Engineers Miss
HART communication requires ≥230Ω total loop impedance.

Most PLC AI cards: 100–150Ω input impedance.

**Add external 100Ω resistor at PLC terminal.** Without it, your HART communicator handshake fails — you can't remote-trim or read diagnostics.

This costs PKR 20 per channel. It's missed on probably 70% of Pakistan HART instrument installations.
