## Day 89 X Post — 2026-09-05

**Post 1 (Thread opener):**
🌞 NEPRA NEM 3.0 changed everything for solar in Pakistan.

New buyers: you're on Net Billing.
Export = PKR 10.69/kWh.
Import = PKR 45–62/kWh.

That's a 5× gap. Here's how to win under the new rules ↓

**Post 2:**
Self-consumption is the game now.

Under NEM 2.0: export earns 1:1 credit.
Under Net Billing: export earns PKR 10/kWh vs PKR 45–62/kWh avoided.

Oversize your system → lose money on exports.
Right-size to your 9am–5pm load → win.

**Post 3:**
Every DISCO has different capacity limits.

LESCO: feeder can absorb 25% DG
PESCO: only 15% — AND a 26-week backlog ⚠️
SEPCO: 10% feeder limit + very limited bi-dir meters

ALWAYS check feeder capacity BEFORE buying panels.

**Post 4:**
The #1 commissioning failure in Pakistan?

Earthing resistance > 5Ω.

Sandy soil in Lahore outskirts, Karachi coastal = high resistivity.
Fix: bentonite-treated pit, extended rods.
Budget: PKR 15,000–40,000 for a proper earth system.

Don't skip this. DISCO inspector will measure.

**Post 5:**
Anti-islanding standard: NEPRA requires IEC 62116.
NOT UL 1741 (that's the US standard).

Many inverters sold at Hafeez Centre: CE mark but NO IEC 62116 type-approval letter.

Ask your installer for the certificate BEFORE buying.
Failed commissioning = reinstall = LOI may expire.
