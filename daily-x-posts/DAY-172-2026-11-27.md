**Thread option (5 tweets):**

1/ Got a chain hoist that keeps tripping the overload? 9/10 times it's not an overload — it's the wrong duty class. CD1 is FEM M3 (800 design hours). Running it 3 shifts? You need MD1 at M5 (3,200 h). 🧵

2/ I-beam sizing mistake we see constantly: calculating for static load only. Add the dynamic factor φ₂ = 1.2–1.3. A 2T hoist on a fast start is actually 2.6T on the beam. IPE-200 at 5-metre span. IPE-240 at 7-metre span. Don't guess — verify deflection ≤ L/750.

3/ Festoon cable tip: loop length between carriages must be 1.5× carriage spacing. Too tight = carriage binding. Too loose = cable dragging. LAPP ÖLFLEX CRANE or equivalent — reeling grade only. Screened cable if you add a VFD (and you should).

4/ Mandatory safety devices under PSQCA lifting gear standards: upper limit switch, overload limiter (110–125% FLC), chain bucket, low-voltage control (24V DC or 42V AC), electromagnetic brake holding 125% SWL with power off. No shortcuts.

5/ VFD on a 3T chain hoist: PKR ~35,000 add-on saves ~PKR 48,000/year in energy + extends brake replacement from twice a year to once every 3 years. Payback < 18 months. Full calculation in the guide. cncelectric.pk
