## Draft 1 (Technical Authority)

🔬 How do you know your 11kV transformer is safe to energise?

You test the insulation. Before every energisation.

HV insulation testing in Pakistan 2026 — what you actually need to know:

📌 IR test voltage must match the asset:
• 500V → LV motors & switchboards
• 2.5kV → 11kV transformers, cables, switchgear
• 5kV → 33kV/132kV assets (NTDC Grid Code 2023 mandatory)
• 10kV → EHV

📌 A basic IR reading isn't enough for WAPDA T&C:
• You need Polarisation Index (PI) — IR at 10 min ÷ IR at 1 min
• PI ≥ 2.0 = good insulation (transformers, motors)
• PI < 1.0 = DO NOT energise

📌 Pakistan's 3 deadliest megger mistakes:
1️⃣ Using a 500V megger on 11kV assets (under-stress = false pass)
2️⃣ Ignoring temperature correction in summer (+50°C oil = 8–12× lower IR reading)
3️⃣ Testing VCB open contacts by IR only (cannot detect vacuum bottle failure)
