## Primary Post

Your panel's busbar is sized for 35°C (IEC reference ambient).

Pakistan summer ambient: 45–48°C.

Correction factor at 45°C = 0.94 → 6% derating minimum.

Add harmonic loading from WAPDA feeders: effective derating can reach 15–18%.

Result: busbar runs hot, insulation degrades, panel fire.

**1/6**
Most Pakistan panel layouts copy a European drawing without adjusting for local conditions. 3 things the IEC standard didn't design for in your panel:
→ 45°C ambient (IEC reference = 35°C)
→ WAPDA ±15% voltage variation
→ 8–18 hr daily load-shedding thermal cycling

**2/6**
VFD mounted below contactors in the same enclosed panel?

VFD heat rises → contactor coil heats to 70–80°C → rated coil life at 40°C halved at every +10°C above rated → 3-year coil life becomes 1.5 years → premature coil burn.

Rule: VFD row is always ABOVE contactor row, or use forced ventilation.

**3/6**
The WAPDA SDO checklist includes one item that rejects most cheap panels:

"Busbar uninsulated on accessible busbars" — EN 50274 IPXXB finger probe requirement.

Every busbar accessible with the door open must be insulated or shrouded so a 12mm test finger cannot reach live parts. Most Pakistani-fabricated panels fail this on first inspection.

**4/6**
24VDC PLC control wiring in the same duct as 400V motor cables?

Capacitive + inductive coupling → false signals on PLC inputs → intermittent trips.

The fault takes 3–5 days to diagnose. The fix (rewiring) costs PKR 80,000–350,000.

Prevention: separate vertical wiring ducts. Cost: PKR 2,000–5,000 extra in materials.

**5/6**
No SPD on WAPDA incomer because "we haven't had a problem."

WAPDA LT switching transients: up to 4 kV on LT network.

One transient destroys: VFD + PLC I/O cards + energy meter + motor protection relay simultaneously.

PKR cost: 200,000–1,500,000. SPD cost: PKR 8,000–25,000.

The math is straightforward.
