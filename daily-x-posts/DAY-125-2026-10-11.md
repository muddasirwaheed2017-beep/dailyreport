## Reply 3 (thread)

Common compliance failure in Pakistan:

No RCD on TT system (rural MEPCO/HESCO supply).

If your supply is single-phase overhead with a consumer earth electrode (TT system), you MUST have a 30 mA RCCB at origin.

Without it: a line-earth fault = live metalwork with no automatic disconnect. Lethal.

Cost: PKR 2,500–4,800 for a 25A 30mA RCCB. Zero excuse.
