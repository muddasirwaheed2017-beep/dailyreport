🏭 New guide: Motor Control Center (MCC) prices in Pakistan 2026

DOL → Star-Delta → Soft Starter → VFD — which starter type does your factory need, and will WAPDA let you use it?

📋 Per-DISCO approval thresholds for all 11 DISCOs:
• LESCO: DOL ok up to 7.5 kW
• SEPCO: DOL only up to 3.7 kW (weaker feeders!)
• PESCO: Star-delta needs approval >22 kW

📦 PKR price range:
• 10-motor fixed MCC: PKR 700K–1.4M
• 30-motor withdrawable Form 4b: PKR 2M–4.5M
• Single VFD feeder unit: PKR 135K–750K

⚠️ Biggest mistake: not getting Icc (short-circuit current) from DISCO before ordering. Wrong busbar rating = failed WAPDA commissioning inspection.
