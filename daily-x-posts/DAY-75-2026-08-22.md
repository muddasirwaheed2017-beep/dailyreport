## Post 2 (Thread — WAPDA angle)

🔌 Pakistan automation fact:

WAPDA voltage drops to 180V during peak load-shedding.

Your PLC power supply handles it (85–264V AC input range).

Your contactors DON'T — they drop out at 180V.

Fix: Run contactor coils from 24V DC SMPS + battery UPS module on DIN rail.
