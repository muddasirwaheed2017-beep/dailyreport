2/ WAPDA/WASA reservoir automation tip:

Ultrasonic (IP67, Modbus RTU) = best bang for PKR at water utilities. 

BUT: for large reservoirs with foam/turbulence → go 26 GHz free-space radar. Sound waves get absorbed; radar doesn't care.

WASA Lahore NRW project using exactly this setup. ✅

3/ Sugar mill molasses tank? Don't use ultrasonic.

Vapour + sticky coating = false empty readings = overflows.

Specify: 26 GHz radar, PTFE antenna, flange DN50, IP68.

Guided wave radar (GWR) also works but rope probe maintenance in molasses = headache.

#SugarMill #PakistanEngineering

4/ Vibrating fork > float switch for overfill protection.

Float switches fail when liquid coats the float ball. Vibrating forks don't care — resonance shift is unaffected by foam, density changes, or coating.

For ETP overflow alarms (ISO 14001 compliance): SIL 2 rated fork is now a requirement at export textile mills. 🏭
