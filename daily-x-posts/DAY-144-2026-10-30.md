**Thread reply 1:**
The hardware watchdog timer is the single most underspecified item in Pakistani SCADA panels.

If the OS freezes after a WAPDA restoration — and it will — a hardware WDT kicks the IPC back to life in 60 seconds without operator intervention.

Software watchdogs don't help when the OS itself is frozen.
