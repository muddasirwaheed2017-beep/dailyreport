## Post 2 (Load-shedding data)

Pakistan load-shedding creates 2,190–4,380 transformer energization events per year.

IEC 60076-7 loading guide assumes 365/year.

In FESCO/MEPCO zones: transformer design life of 25–30 years collapses to 12–16 years.

Fix: inhibited oil (Type II IEC 60296) + Buchholz relay. PKR 25,000 saves PKR 1.5M+.

## Post 3 (Tap changer zone table)

Wrong tap changer setting = chronic undervoltage. Pakistan DISCO zone guide:

🔴 MEPCO/PESCO: +5% to +7.5% (feeder drops to 9.5kV in summer)
🟡 LESCO/IESCO: +2.5%
🟢 K-Electric: 0% or -2.5% (K-E runs HIGH voltage intentionally)

Running MEPCO feeder at 0% tap → 358V LT → 25% excess motor current → early motor failure.
