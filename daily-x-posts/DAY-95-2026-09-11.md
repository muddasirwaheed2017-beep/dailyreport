## Thread Reply 1

Risk #1: Post-loadshedding feeder restoration

When DISCO restores a feeder, 10-20 transformers energise simultaneously.

Cumulative inrush creates a 2nd harmonic profile that drops BELOW the 87T blocking threshold (15%).

Result: 87T trips a healthy transformer.

This has happened 6 documented times in FESCO and MEPCO zones in 2024-2025.

Fix: Cross-block (adaptive 2nd harmonic) mode. Or staggered energisation sequence.

## Thread Reply 2

Risk #6: Protection blind window during loadshedding

Most panel protection relays are powered from the same LV busbar they're protecting.

When loadshedding cuts supply → relay DC supply fails.

When supply restores → relay takes 0.5–2.0s to restart.

Fault during that window = no protection.

4 transformer failures traced to this cause in NTDC's 2024 fault database.

Fix: Battery-backed auxiliary supply with ≥30 min backup. NTDC Grid Code Section 8.4.3 requires this but it's rarely specified.
