## Post 1 — K-Electric TT Shock Risk

**Using MCB alone on K-Electric supply? That's a life-safety violation.**

K-Electric runs TT earthing at service entry.
Fault loop = 150–400Ω total.
Phase-to-earth fault current = 0.5–1.5A.

Your 16A MCB will NOT trip.
The fault continues. Touch voltage = 50–100V. Duration = indefinite.

Only a 30mA RCD trips on 30mA regardless of fault impedance.

RCD on K-Electric = mandatory, not optional.

Full guide: cncelectric.pk/blogs/guides/rcd-rccb-rcbo-...

## Post 2 — WAPDA Load-Shedding RCCB Trip Fix

Load-shedding restoration keeps tripping your 30mA RCCB?

Here's why: WAPDA voltage step 0V→220V in 40–80ms charges cable capacitance.
Inrush = 8–18mA capacitive leakage for 5–15ms.
Instantaneous 30mA RCCB trips. No actual fault exists.

Fix: S-type (time-delayed) RCCB at board incomer
→ 100mA, 130ms delay
→ Rides through the transient
→ 30mA branch RCBOs still protect you

One-time upgrade. End of nuisance trips.

## Post 3 — Solar Inverter + Type AC RCCB = Silent Fault Miss

90% of Pakistani solar installations use Type AC RCCBs.
Problem: your inverter produces pulsating DC fault current.
Type AC detects AC sine-wave faults only.

Result: real earth fault on your solar system → RCCB doesn't trip.

NEPRA NEM 2023 Amendment 2, Clause 9.4:
Type A RCCB mandatory on all solar inverter AC outputs.

Price difference: ~PKR 3,000 per device.
Cost of a missed fault: incalculable.

Upgrade now before NEPRA DG inspection.
