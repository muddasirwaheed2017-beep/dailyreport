## Post

Your 100 kVA UPS keeps switching to battery on generator supply?

Most likely cause: your generator is 100-120 kVA for a 100 kVA UPS.

That's 1:1 sizing. You need 2:1 minimum for standard rectifiers.

Why: UPS rectifiers draw 250-350% inrush current at startup. A standard generator can't absorb that without voltage dip to 70-75%... triggering UPS battery transfer.

Fix options:
→ Upsize generator (2×-2.5× kVA)
→ Install AFE rectifier UPS (only 1.1× needed)
→ Add 5% AC line reactor as interim fix

Pakistan-specific: add 10-15% extra for 45°C ambient derating.
