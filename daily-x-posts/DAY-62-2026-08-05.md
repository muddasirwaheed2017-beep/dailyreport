## Post 2 (Seasonal resistivity hook)

📊 Pakistan soil fact engineers miss:

Central Punjab (Lahore/Faisalabad) soil resistivity:
- August monsoon: 20–60 Ω·m → electrode reads 4 Ω ✅
- May dry season: 100–300 Ω·m → same electrode reads 35 Ω ❌

WAPDA requires ≤10 Ω. Always test in April–June.
