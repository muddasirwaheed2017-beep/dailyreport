### Post 2 — Pakistan-specific problem hook (load-shedding)

Pakistan textile mills on 12-hour load-shedding: belt life drops 3–4× vs stable power.

Root cause: DOL restart across-the-line = 5–7× running torque shock on every restart.

Fix: Pakistan service factor add-on +0.15–0.25 for ≥4 restarts/day. Same rule as shaft couplings.
