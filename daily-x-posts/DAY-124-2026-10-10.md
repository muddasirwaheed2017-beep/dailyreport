## Reply 1 (thread)

What's the grading margin between upstream and downstream breakers?

0.3–0.4 seconds minimum.

Breakdown:
- CB opening time: 0.05–0.08 s
- Relay overtravel: 0.05 s
- CT error + tolerance: 0.05–0.10 s
- Safety buffer: 0.10–0.15 s

Miss this margin → upstream trips instead → your whole floor goes dark.

## Reply 2 (thread)

Zone Selective Interlocking (ZSI) solves the speed vs selectivity tradeoff.

How:
- Downstream fault → sends restraint signal upstream
- Upstream adds 60–100 ms delay → downstream clears
- No downstream signal → upstream trips INSTANTLY (<20 ms)

ZSI retrofit kits for MCCBs: PKR 35,000–90,000
Worth every rupee for a 1 MVA industrial panel.
