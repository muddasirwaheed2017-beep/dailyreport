**Tweet 2:**
The PA6 vs PA66 cable tie fraud in Pakistan:

Most ties sold as "PA66 nylon 66" on local market are actually PA6 — lower heat rating, weaker UV resistance, fails on rooftop solar at Lahore/Karachi 43°C summer.

4-check identification protocol:
✅ Weight test (PA66 = denser)
✅ Flame test (PA66 self-extinguishes faster)
✅ 90°C water test (PA6 softens; PA66 firm)
✅ UL94 V-2 marking + explicit "PA66" on pack

**Tweet 3:**
DANGER alert for Pakistan distribution contractors:

PVC insulation tape is rated 600V max.

Using it on 11kV or 33kV cable damage = live flashover risk.

HV self-fusing silicone tape (33kV rated) for emergency. Follow with IEC 60840 joint kit within 72h.

Never tape an MV cable with PVC tape.
