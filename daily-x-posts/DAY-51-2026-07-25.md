### Tweet 2 (The 45°C Derating Rule)
IEC busbar ratings are at 35°C ambient.

Pakistan switchrooms hit 40–50°C in summer.

45°C derating formula:
I_derated = I_rated × √((85−45)/(85−35)) = 0.894

Translation: your "400A busbar" only carries 358A in Pakistan conditions.

For 350A design load → you need 500A IEC-rated busbar → 63×6mm copper.

---

### Tweet 3 (4 Feeder Types)
Which starter goes in which MCC compartment?

🟢 DOL — small motors ≤7.5kW, short start, low inertia. Simple.

🟡 Star-Delta — 11–75kW Pakistan factory standard. Reduces starting current to 33%. DANGER: timer must be 6–10 seconds. Short timer = worse than DOL start.

🔵 VFD — fans, HVAC, precision conveyors. Never switch on VFD output side while running.

🟠 Soft Starter — centrifugal pumps, compressors. MUST have bypass contactor or soft starter fails in 18 months.

---

### Tweet 4 (The Bypass Contactor Rule)
Most common Pakistan soft starter mistake:

No bypass contactor installed.

Soft starter handles full motor current continuously → thyristors overheat → failure in 6–18 months.

Bypass contactor closes after motor reaches full speed. Soft starter then carries zero current.

Cost of missing it: PKR 55,000–150,000 per replacement soft starter.

---

### Tweet 5 (PLC to MCC — Never Direct Wire)
🚫 Wrong: PLC output → 230VAC contactor coil direct.

The inductive kick on coil de-energisation destroys transistor output cards. Pakistan load-shedding makes this happen 8–20× per day.

✅ Right: PLC output → 24VDC interposing relay → 230VAC contactor coil.

Interposing relay cost: PKR 400–700 each.
PLC output module replacement: PKR 15,000–45,000 each.

The maths is obvious.

---

### Tweet 6 (Single-Phasing — Pakistan's #1 Motor Killer)
WAPDA single-phasing (1 phase drops) for 2–30 minutes is the leading cause of Pakistan motor burnouts.

Thermal overload relay is slow to respond at light loads.

Phase failure relay trips instantly — under-voltage AND asymmetry detection.

Settings for WAPDA supply:
- UV: 330V
- OV: 430V
- Asymmetry: 3–4%
- Time delay: 2–5 seconds

Install one on every MCC feeder. Motor rewind cost: PKR 15,000–120,000.
