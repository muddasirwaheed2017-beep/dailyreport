## Thread 1: Compressor Short-Cycling Problem

A Pakistani factory's 11 kW compressor failed its motor starter in 3 months.

Root cause: 0.5 bar hysteresis on a switch in a 500 L tank → 32 cycles/hour.

The fix is free: wider hysteresis setpoint OR bigger receiver tank.

## Thread 2: DP Switch for Filter Monitoring

Pakistan factories change compressed air filters on calendar schedules.

A DP switch across the filter tells you when it's actually clogged — not a month after or a month before.

Set at 0.5 bar ΔP = one full 8–10 hour working day of warning.

Cost of DP switch: PKR 5,500–22,000.
Cost of a burnt compressor from undetected filter clog: ask your maintenance department.

## Thread 3: SPDT NC Wiring — The Safety Rule

Every pressure/flow switch in a Pakistan protection circuit must be wired through the NC contact.

Wire break = PLC input de-energises = same as low-pressure alarm = protective action.

NO contact wiring: wire break = "no alarm" → dangerous fail state.

One wiring decision. The difference between safe and not.

## Thread 4: Thermal Flow Switch for Pakistan Compressed Air

Paddle flow switches in compressed air lines fail in months (vibration + condensate + oil).

Thermal insertion flow switches: no moving parts, ¾" BSP tapping, work for years.

PKR 12,000–22,000 vs a weekend of downtime to find a failed paddle and replace it.
