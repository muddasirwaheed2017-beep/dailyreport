**Post 1 (primary):**

Building a CNC router or laser cutter in Pakistan?

Don't size your stepper motors by "it looks right" 🧵

NEMA 17 vs 23 vs 34 — when each wins:
→ NEMA 17: 3D printer, 40W laser, foam CNC
→ NEMA 23: CNC router X/Y, 100W CO₂ laser, plasma gantry
→ NEMA 34: Large gantry, stone engraver, heavy aluminium milling

Driver PKR 2026 prices:
• TB6600 clone: PKR 800–1,800
• DM542T (Leadshine OEM): PKR 2,200–4,500
• DM860H: PKR 4,500–8,000
• AM882 closed-loop: PKR 12,000–22,000

Pakistan-specific warning ⚡:
Pakistan grid swings 200–260V → SMPS output can spike.
DM542T max is 50 VDC. Run 36 VDC nominal — not 48 V.
At 48 V nominal, grid spikes kill capacitors within months.

**Post 3 (tip):**

Pakistan CNC tip 🇵🇰:

Running stepper motors in a Sialkot/Faisalabad workshop this summer?

→ Derate your driver current by 10–15% above 40°C ambient
→ Add a 24V panel fan (PKR 300–600) if machine runs >4 hours/day
→ Home switches on all axes = load-shedding recovery without manual reset

Missed steps in a hot, dusty workshop cost more in scrap than the fan.
