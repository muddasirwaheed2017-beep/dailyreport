## Primary Post (thread)

🖥️ **HMI panels for Pakistani industry — what you need to know in 2026**

Running a textile mill in Faisalabad? Sugar plant in Sindh? Water treatment in Karachi?

Your HMI is the face of your whole control system. Here's a quick Pakistan-specific guide 👇

---

**Thread 1/5** — Brownout protection

WAPDA drops to 185V (or 160V rural) in summer. Your HMI power supply MUST handle this.

✅ 24VDC DIN-rail SMPS (85–265VAC input) feeding HMI DC port
✅ 12V/7Ah SLA battery for generator changeover blackout

Rs 8,000–15,000 extra. Worth every rupee.

---

**Thread 2/5** — Screen size guide

4.3" → pump panel, DG AMF (Rs 18–28K)
7" → textile MCC, water treatment (Rs 45–130K) ← MOST POPULAR
10" → sugar mill, WAPDA bay (Rs 95–220K)
15" → SCADA workstation (Rs 380–650K)

7" widescreen = Pakistan sweet spot 🎯

---

**Thread 3/5** — MODBUS wiring mistakes

Every HMI supports MODBUS RTU RS-485. Common Pakistan panel builder mistakes:

#1: No 120-ohm termination at cable ends
#2: Unshielded cable over 20m
#3: RS-485 cable next to 440V power cable

---

**Thread 4/5** — Customs reality

~50% effective duty burden in Pakistan (HS 8537.10). EUR 450 panel ≈ Rs 200–210K landed.

EOUs under DTRE/SRO 450 → duty exempt. Ask your clearing agent.
