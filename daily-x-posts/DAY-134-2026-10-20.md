### Post 2 (Pakistan load-shedding problem)
Pakistan engineers: standard ANSI service factor isn't enough for your chain drives.

Load-shedding DOL restarts deliver 6–8× rated torque into the chain every time power returns.

4–6 restarts/day? Add +0.25 to your service factor.
8+ restarts/day? Go double-strand or next series up.
