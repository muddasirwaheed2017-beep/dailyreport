## Thread Post 1

OPC-DA vs OPC-UA — why your S7-300 installation needs the upgrade:

OPC-DA (Classic):
- Windows DCOM only — random firewall ports
- No encryption, NTLM auth
- Dies with Windows 7/Server 2008 end-of-life

OPC-UA (IEC 62541):
- Single TCP port (4840) — firewall-friendly
- X.509 certificate auth + AES-256 encryption
- Runs on Linux, embedded, Raspberry Pi
- Works across factory → cloud without VPN hacks

The legacy Siemens MPI adapter keeping your OPC-DA alive is one Windows EOL away from leaving you with no SCADA connection.

## Thread Post 2

The Purdue Model — why Pakistani factories need to stop ignoring it:

Most factories in Pakistan:
- PLC network + office PC + SCADA server + internet router = ONE flat switch
- Ransomware on the director's laptop → PLC in 10 seconds

Purdue Model minimum viable for Pakistan:
- Level 0-2 (field + PLC + SCADA): isolated VLAN on managed switch
- DMZ: OPC-UA reverse connect through firewall
- Level 4 (IT/ERP): separate network entirely

Cost of a VLAN-capable managed industrial switch: PKR 35,000–65,000
Cost of a ransomware shutdown for 3 days: PKR 2,000,000+

The math is easy.
