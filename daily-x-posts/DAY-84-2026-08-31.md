### Post 2 — The 6 failure modes ranked
Pakistan underground cable joint failures by cause:

1️⃣ Contaminated insulation surface (28%) — jointer touched XLPE without gloves
2️⃣ Wrong screen cut position (21%) — field measurement error
3️⃣ Jointing in monsoon rain / humidity (17%)
4️⃣ Wrong kit — XLPE kit on PILC cable (14%)
5️⃣ Under-crimped connector (11%)
6️⃣ Joint moved before cure complete (9%)

Every one is preventable. Every one shows up in LESCO/K-Electric incident logs.
