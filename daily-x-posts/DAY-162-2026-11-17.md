### Tweet 2 (The Problem)
DC current is fundamentally different from AC.

AC has 100 zero-crossings per second (50Hz × 2). The arc self-extinguishes at each one.

DC has ZERO zero-crossings.

A 600V DC arc will sustain indefinitely until:
✅ The gap is physically large enough
✅ A DC-rated MCB extinguishes it with blow-out magnets

That's why your DC MCB costs more than an AC one. The magnets and larger contact gap are doing real work.

### Tweet 3 (String Fuses)
Most Pakistani solar installers skip string fuses. Here's why that's dangerous:

If you have 2+ strings in parallel and ONE string shades out →
↓ That string's current drops
↓ Other strings push current backwards through it
↓ Your string cable (inside conduit, invisible) catches fire

The fix: gPV fuses (IEC 60269-6) in a combiner box.
Cost: PKR 180–350 per fuse.
Consequence of skipping: PKR 50,000+ cable replacement + possible roof fire.
