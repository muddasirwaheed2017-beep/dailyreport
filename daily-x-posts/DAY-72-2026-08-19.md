## Post 3 (K-factor education)

⚡ Fitting a standard K-1 transformer on your VFD panel or server room UPS is a slow death sentence for the transformer.

Non-linear loads (VFDs, SMPS, UPS rectifiers) need:
• K-4 for mild non-linear loads
• K-13 for CNC/VFD panels
• K-20 for UPS rectifiers

Standard transformers overheat in 2–4 years. Specify the right K-factor.
