**LED street lights in Pakistan: the numbers that matter 👇**

Replace 150W HPSV with 60W LED on 1km of road:
→ WAPDA saving: PKR 381,743/km/year
→ Payback: 15 months
→ Add generator fuel saving (HESCO/QESCO zones): PKR 532,224/km/year
→ Combined payback: under 7 months

Pakistan has 2.8 million streetlight points still running HPSV.
National saving potential if all converted: PKR 7.5–10 billion/year.

Key spec for Pakistani conditions:
✅ IP66+IK08 (not IP65 — monsoon Sindh/Punjab requires IP66)
✅ Ta=55°C driver (Meanwell ELG/XLG — not generic Ta≤45°C)
✅ PSQCA PS 4778:2022 certified (customs interception active 2026)
✅ Asymmetric Type II/III optics (not symmetric — EN 13201 compliance)

Solar street lights: only LiFePO4 battery survives 45°C Pakistan ambient.
SLA lead-acid fails in 18 months. Always verify chemistry on spec sheet.
