## Draft 2 — The Sub-Metering Stack

5 layers every Pakistani factory EMS needs:

L1 — Main incomer (Class 0.5S, CT-operated): PKR 18K–65K
L2 — Sub-distribution per department: PKR 4.5K–12K
L3 — Major loads (drives, compressors): PKR 2.5K–8K
L4 — Gas/diesel/water (pulse or M-Bus): PKR 15K–90K
L5 — IIoT gateway (Modbus → MQTT/OPC-UA): PKR 22K–85K

No L5 = no EMS. The gateway is the nervous system.
