### Post 3 — Pakistan-specific insight

🌡️ Your VCB is rated at 40°C. Multan switchgear rooms hit 55°C in summer.

At 55°C, a 630A VCB delivers only **542A** effective.

If your transformer load is 580A at peak summer — you're running the VCB overloaded.

Fix: Specify "Ith at 45°C" on the purchase order. Forces the factory to declare derated value.
