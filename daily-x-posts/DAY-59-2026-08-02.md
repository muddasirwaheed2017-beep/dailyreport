## Thread (6 posts)

**1/**
🚨 Most Pakistani industrial cable tray fails in 5–8 years.

Not from wear. From 3 engineering errors.

Here's what no Pakistani product catalogue documents 🧵

**2/**
Error 1: Pre-galvanized tray at Karachi coastal sites.

Z275 tray fails in 3–5 years at Karachi's salt + SO₂ environment.

By the time rust is visible, cable insulation is already compromised.

Cost to fix in a running facility: 8–12× the original installed cost.

✅ Use hot-dip galvanized (HDG ≥85µm) minimum. PVC-coated or GRP for Port Qasim/Gwadar.

**3/**
Error 2: 40% fill ratio on Pakistan summer outdoor routes.

IEC 61537 calculates at 40°C reference.

Pakistan outdoor tray reaches 55–60°C in summer.

Corrected fill ratio table:
→ Indoor A/C: 40% (standard)
→ Covered outdoor 45–50°C: 28%
→ Exposed rooftop 55–60°C: 22%
→ Cement/sugar plant rooftop: 18% max

Ignoring this → cable life drops from 30 years to under 3.

**4/**
Error 3: Power + protection cables in same tray at WAPDA substations.

WAPDA Substation Design Manual: minimum 300mm separation between HV cables and protection/metering cables.

Most common violation: single tray with a thin divider.

WAPDA SDO rejects it.

Remediation after completion: PKR 180,000–750,000.

**5/**
Ladder vs Perforated: the one-line rule.

✅ Power feeders ≥35mm²: LADDER (open top = full convection at Pakistan's 45–60°C ambient)
✅ Control/instrument cables: PERFORATED (continuous floor, clean routing)

Perforated retains 30–40% more heat than ladder.

Using perforated for power feeders in Pakistan summer = derating problem.
