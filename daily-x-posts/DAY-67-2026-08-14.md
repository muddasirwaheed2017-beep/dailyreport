**Tweet 3:**
Wiring duct in Pakistani panel shops — the 50°C rule nobody follows:

IEC sizing at 40°C ambient. Multan summer = 50°C ambient.

Go ONE duct size larger than calculated.
40×40mm → use 40×60mm.
40×60mm → use 60×60mm.

Prevents insulation softening on packed wires. ✅
