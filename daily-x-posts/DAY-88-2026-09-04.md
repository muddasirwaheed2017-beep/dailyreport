## Thread Post 1/3 — MPPT Specs

The MPPT "99.9% efficiency" claim is marketing. What matters in Pakistan:

📍 String Voc × winter temp correction must be ≤95% of inverter Max PV Voltage
📍 Vmpp at 60°C cell temp must be ≥ MPPT minimum × 1.10 buffer
📍 Euro Efficiency (≥96.5%) beats peak efficiency spec for annual yield

Lahore monsoon + 50W/m² start-up threshold = 8–12% more yield vs 150W/m² inverters.

## Thread Post 2/3 — NEPRA 2025 Change

NEPRA's July 2025 update changed the game for battery-coupled systems:

BMS must PROVE it can't discharge battery into grid (no off-peak buy/peak sell arbitrage allowed)

This means: any hybrid inverter for net metering needs a BMS with separate self-consumption mode — not all do.

Check the NEPRA approved equipment list before buying.
