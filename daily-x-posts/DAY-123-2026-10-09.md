### Thread Reply 2

The Icu vs Ics trap Pakistan engineers overlook:

Icu = max fault current (one time — then replace the breaker)
Ics = fault current you can interrupt and keep running

Ics 50% Icu at the wrong price point = every fault costs you TWO MCCBs.

Specify Ics = 100% Icu for service entrances. ⚡

### Thread Reply 3

Why does your MCCB trip in summer but not winter in Pakistan?

IEC 60947-2 calibrates at 40°C. Your LT room hits 50°C in August.
At 50°C → 20–25% derating.
A 400A MCCB in that room is effectively a 300–320A device.

Size frames with ambient derating, not nameplate alone.
