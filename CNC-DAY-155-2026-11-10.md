NEPRA's 2024 DSM Regulations also open a new capacity payment revenue stream for factories registering as Demand Response Providers. No deep-technical Pakistani buyer's guide for ADR controllers exists covering both WAPDA tariff optimisation AND NEPRA DSM registration. This article fills a unique cluster-capping content gap, targeting facility engineers and energy managers who have an EMS and PQDR (from D153 and D154) and need the demand management automation layer to complete the energy optimisation stack.
