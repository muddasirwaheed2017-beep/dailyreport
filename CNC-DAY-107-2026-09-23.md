**Pakistan-specific differentiators (7 unique):**
1. WAPDA/DISCO IEEE 519-2022 enforcement gap — NEPRA Determination 2025 adds harmonic compliance for >500kW C1/C2/D2 consumers, pending penalty finalisation
2. VFD density in textile/cement — Faisalabad/Karachi >60% VFD penetration causes 7–12% THDv at 11kV MCC busbars (1.4–2.4× IEEE 519 limit before filtering)
3. Generator island-mode amplification — 12–16 hrs/day loadshedding, generator source impedance 8–15× WAPDA, THDv can reach 30–65% on generator bus vs 8% on WAPDA supply
4. K-factor transformer misapplication — K-1 standard transformers sold universally in Pakistan; VFD-dominated MCCs impose K-4 to K-13, causing premature failure in 8–12 years instead of 25+
5. NEPRA Determination 2025 — pending PKR/kVAh harmonic penalty for >500kW C1/C2/D2 consumers; pre-inspection baseline measurement urgency
6. IEEE 519-2022 two-point measurement methodology — PCC + load terminal simultaneous measurement for consumer vs grid contribution attribution and DISCO dispute resolution
7. Summer peak WAPDA grid weakness (Jun–Sep) — reduced spinning reserve + disconnected shunt capacitors raises source impedance 30–50%, amplifying THDv 1.5–2× vs winter baseline
