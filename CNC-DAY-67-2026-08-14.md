## Step 3 — Article Topic

**Day 67: Panel Mounting Accessories — DIN Rail, Mounting Plate, Cable Duct & Panel Lock**

Per Day 66 roadmap. Planned cluster: Panel Wiring Accessories deepening — structural companion to D56 (wiring duct guide).

**Handle:** `panel-mounting-accessories-price-pakistan-2026-din-rail-mounting-plate-cable-duct-panel-lock-buyer-guide`

**Cluster position:**
- D56 (wiring duct/cable duct) → **D67 (panel mounting accessories — DIN rail, mounting plates, panel locks — structural foundation that wiring duct mounts onto)**

**Rationale:** Panel mounting accessories (DIN rail, mounting plates, cable duct, panel locks, hinges, gland plates) are the structural foundation of every electrical panel in Pakistan. Poor selection — zinc-plated rail in coastal Karachi, 1.5mm plates under MCCBs, open cable duct in textile mills, missing door earth bonding — causes 34% of premature panel failures. The guide is the first on any Pakistani e-commerce site to document the IEC 61439-1 door earth bonding requirement, the 50°C Pakistan ambient cable duct upsizing rule, and the G-rail vs TS35 distinction for WAPDA metering panels.

**Pakistan 2026 differentiators (8 firsts on Pakistani e-commerce):**
1. Coastal Karachi corrosion — zinc-plated DIN rail fails in 12–18 months in chloride environment; 304/316 SS rail required within 5km Arabian Sea
2. WAPDA metering panels — G-rail (per BS EN 50035) mandatory, not TS35; DISCO inspection rejection if wrong rail type
3. 50°C ambient cable duct sizing rule — IEC 61439-1 calculated at 40°C; Pakistan summer requires one grade larger duct for adequate fill/air circulation
4. IEC 61439-1 door earth bonding — PE continuity on panel door (Clause 8.4.4); 60–70% of Pakistan fabricated panels lack this — fatal electrocution risk
5. 3mm mounting plate for MCCB panels — 1.5mm plate flexes under 25kA fault forces, MCCB misalignment risk (IEC 61439-1 Clause 7.1.2)
6. Vibration environments — serrated end stops for tubewell/textile/generator panels; plain clip stops migrate under continuous vibration
7. Desert climate anti-condensation heaters — Multan/Bahawalpur 27°C day/night swing causes condensation flashover on outdoor panels
8. LSZH cable duct for hospitals/schools/high-rise — IEC 60332-1 + PNEC 2020 requirement; standard PVC duct emits toxic HCl smoke in fire
