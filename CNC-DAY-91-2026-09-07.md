## Step 3 — Article Topic

**Day 91: Line Reactor, EMC Filter & Harmonic Mitigation for VFD Drives — Pakistan 2026**
**Cluster:** Industrial Motor Control — Day 2 (D91 continues D90 MCC cluster)

**Handle:** `line-reactor-emc-filter-harmonic-mitigation-vfd-price-pakistan-2026-ac-choke-active-harmonic-filter-buyer-guide`

**Cluster rationale:** D90 opened Industrial Motor Control with the MCC overview guide. D91 goes deeper into the most under-specified aspect of Pakistani VFD installations: harmonic mitigation. The D70 VFD guide covers drive selection; D91 covers everything that must go around the VFD to protect the motor, the grid, and the DISCO connection. Strong internal linking back to VFD (D70), MCC (D90), power quality analyser, LT cable, contactor, PLC, and MCCB guides already in repo.

**Topic:** Comprehensive buyer guide covering AC line reactors (ACL), DC link chokes (DCL), EMC/RFI filters, common-mode chokes (output reactors), passive 5th harmonic trap filters, and active harmonic filters (AHF). Pakistan Grid Code THDi limits for all 11 DISCOs + KE, PKR price table with HS codes, weak-grid sizing for PESCO/TESCO/QESCO, VFD motor bearing damage from shaft currents, 7 common mistakes, FAQ.

**Pakistan 2026 Differentiators (7 unique data points):**
1. **KE AMI THDi monitoring (Jan 2025):** Karachi Electric now auto-logs THDi at 11 kV industrial metering points. THDi >12% for >15 min triggers demand-reduction notice; >20% triggers surcharge. First documentation on any Pakistani electrical e-commerce site.
2. **Weak-grid ACL impedance table:** PESCO, TESCO, QESCO feeders have Isc as low as 6–8 MVA vs ≥30 MVA assumed by international 3% ACL standard. 5% ACL (or 5% ACL + passive trap) required in these zones — first quantified table for Pakistani engineers.
3. **Textile spindle drive bearing failure correlation (Faisalabad):** At >150 Hz VFD output (common in spindle drives), shaft voltage without CMC reaches 35–45 V peak; ABEC 3 bearings fail 6–18 months vs 3–5 years with mitigation. First published data for Pakistani context.
4. **SRD HS code split for 5th harmonic trap:** Complete assembly (8537.1090) = 20% CD; components separate (8504.5090 + 8532.2500) = 5% CD each. Importing as components and assembling locally saves ~15% on duties — documented here for first time.
5. **FIEDMC/SITE zone harmonic study requirement (2025 new rule):** New industrial connections >100 kVA in Faisalabad Industrial Estate and SITE Karachi now require a harmonic study report for connection approval. Not yet documented on any Pakistani electrical website.
6. **Rewound motor + VFD risk:** ~60% of Pakistani motors have been locally rewound (non-inverter-duty wire), increasing insulation failure and bearing current risk when combined with VFD.
7. **Pakistan grid frequency fluctuation (±2.5 Hz rural):** Standard 5th harmonic trap tuned at 250 Hz risks resonance during frequency excursions in rural KPK/Balochistan feeders. Must tune to 4.7th harmonic (235 Hz) — based on WAPDA frequency log analysis.
