**The number that matters most:**

A 37kW centrifugal pump running at 80% speed with a VFD consumes only 51% of its full-speed energy.

At WAPDA B-2 tariff (PKR 32/kWh), that translates to:
- Annual saving: **PKR 3,475,200**
- VFD cost: **PKR 290,000–390,000**
- Simple payback: **31–41 days**

Not months. Not years. **Days.**

**Why is Pakistan's VFD adoption still below 18%?**

Three reasons I hear consistently:
1. Upfront cost hesitation (ROI calculation fixes this)
2. WAPDA supply quality concerns (solved with input line reactor + proper earthing)
3. Fear of harmonic interaction with existing cap banks (solved with de-tuning reactor)

**What the NEECA SRO 1065 mandate changes:**

The IE3 motor requirement for new installations above 7.5kW is live. But IE3 alone saves only ~3% over IE1 on a fixed-speed DOL drive. Add a VFD and that saving jumps to **50–70%**. The two technologies are designed to work together — the mandate is the catalyst.
