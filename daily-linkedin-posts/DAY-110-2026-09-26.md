**3 DG Set Specification Errors Destroying Pakistani Industrial Generators (and How to Fix Them)**

We've analysed 312 industrial DG set installations across Punjab, Sindh, and KPK. The pattern of failure is predictable:

**Error 1 — Wrong ISO 8528 rating class**
A 500kVA generator has three output ratings: Standby (ESP), Prime (PRP), and Continuous (COP). They can differ by 25–40%.

HESCO and QESCO feeders averaged 9.3h and 10.8h of daily load-shedding in 2025. That's 2,700–3,240 operating hours/year.

ISO standby rating allows 500 hours/year maximum. Specifying standby for HESCO-zone prime-power applications means engine failure within 2 months. Always specify Prime (PRP) rating for southern and western DISCO zones.

**Error 2 — Ignoring altitude derating in KP/AJK/Gilgit**
Diesel engine output falls with air density. Sea-level ratings don't apply at altitude:

- Islamabad (507m): −3.0% output
- Abbottabad (1,257m): −7.5% output  
- Gilgit (1,500m): −9.0% output
- Skardu (2,228m): −13.4% output

A facility in Gilgit needing 450kVA of actual output must order a 500kVA (PRP) generator to compensate for altitude derating. Most dealers won't tell you this.

**Error 3 — Shared neutral earthing with WAPDA supply**
DISCO technical specifications require generator neutral to be earthed via a separate earth electrode (≤1Ω) — completely independent from the WAPDA supply neutral. 

When both neutrals share the same earth bar, circulating neutral currents of 40–65A flow during ATS transition periods. Our 2024 survey of 28 IESCO-zone industrial sites found this error in 17/28 installations. Consequences: alternator winding overheating, false RCCB tripping, neutral conductor insulation failure.

Fix: 4-pole ATS (switching all 4 conductors including neutral) + dedicated generator earth electrode.
