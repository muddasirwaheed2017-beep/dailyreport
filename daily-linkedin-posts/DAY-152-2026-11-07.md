**Industrial SIEM & OT SOC Pakistan 2026 — The Visibility Gap Costing Factories Their Uptime**

Most Pakistani industrial facilities have invested in OT network security in 2025–2026: an IEC 62443-compliant firewall with zone segmentation, a Zero-Trust VPN for remote maintenance, hardened PLCs and HMIs. These are the right moves.

But there is a critical missing layer: **visibility**. The firewall generates logs. The VPN generates logs. The historian generates events. The PLC generates alarms. None of these flows are reaching a Security Operations Centre that can correlate them, detect lateral movement, and trigger a CERT-PK incident report within 24 hours.

This is the gap an OT SIEM fills.
