**Motor Protection Relay Pakistan 2026: Why Bimetal TOLs Fail on WAPDA Supply — and What to Use Instead**

Three 15kW pump motors burned in one month at a Lahore industrial estate.

Root cause: basic bimetal thermal overload relays that took 6–8 minutes to trip on single-phasing. The motor insulation reached its thermal limit in under 4 minutes.

The fix cost PKR 11,500 per motor. The motors it protected were worth PKR 85,000–95,000 each.

---

**Pakistan's WAPDA supply creates four specific motor threats that standard European-spec protection devices are not configured for:**

1. **Phase loss (single phasing)** — WAPDA 11kV feeders in industrial estates experience 2–5 phase-loss events per month in peak summer. A basic bimetal TOL can take 3–8 minutes to trip at light load. Electronic overload relays trip in under 2 seconds.

2. **Voltage imbalance** — LV feeders commonly run at 3–8% imbalance from unequal single-phase loading. At 5% imbalance, negative-sequence current heats the rotor at 6× the rated loss rate.

3. **Deep voltage sag** — Rural and peri-urban feeders sag to 340–360V during peak hours. At 360V, motor current rises 12% above FLA. Without undervoltage protection, the motor stalls and draws locked-rotor current.

4. **High ambient temperature** — Pakistani factory floors hit 45–50 °C in summer. For every 10 °C above rated ambient, motor life is halved (Arrhenius law). A bimetal TOL set to rated FLA in a 50 °C panel is under-protecting the motor.

---

**Selection rule for Pakistan:**

| Motor size | Protection choice |
|---|---|
| ≤ 5.5kW, agricultural, budget | Phase-loss sensitive bimetal TOL, Class 10 |
| 5.5–45kW, industrial pumps, compressors | Electronic overload relay (EOL) with thermistor |
| > 15kW, critical process, generator-fed | Full motor protection relay (MPR) with Modbus RTU |
| VFD-fed motors | VFD internal protection + PTC thermistor input — no TOL on VFD output |
