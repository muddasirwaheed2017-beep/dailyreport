**Why oil testing in Pakistan is harder than IEC literature assumes:**
- 41% of FESCO's 11kV DTs failed BDV after the 2023 monsoon (sub-standard breathers + deteriorated gaskets)

**DGA — the tool most underused on the distribution network:**
Pakistan's DGA culture is concentrated on 132kV+ transformers. Yet 6 of 2024's most costly DISCO transformer fires occurred on 11kV units with no DGA history. A full DGA at a PNAC lab costs PKR 12,000–22,000. The transformer costs PKR 1.5–4 million to replace (and that's before the outage cost).
