# LinkedIn Post — Day 167 — 2026-11-22

**Title:** Pakistan's EV Charging Gap Is a PKR 25–40 Billion Opportunity — But Software Is the Bottleneck

---

Pakistan has 420 operational public EV charge points today.

NTRC's target: 4,000 by 2028.

That 3,580-unit gap represents PKR 25–40 billion in infrastructure investment — and it's not being held back by demand. Pakistan's EV registrations crossed 350,000 two- and three-wheelers and 18,000 cars in 2026. The demand is real.

**The bottleneck is operational software.**

Most early CPOs in Pakistan launched with charger-bundled CPMS platforms — proprietary, locked-in, with no OCPI roaming capability and no demand charge management. Result: stranded assets earning 40–50% less than they should.

**Here's the financial case for OCPP 2.0.1 + enterprise CPMS:**

A 6-EVSE urban site (Lahore mall) under our worked example:
• Year 1 Revenue: PKR 18.7M (at 20% utilisation)
• Year 1 Gross Profit: PKR 6.9M (37% margin)
• Year 2 Gross Profit: PKR 12.1M at 35% utilisation
• Simple payback: ~24 months

**Three things that move the needle most:**

1. **OCPP 2.0.1 DLM** — dynamic load management prevents demand charge spikes. At a 160kW site, this alone saves PKR 1.2–1.8M/year vs unmanaged charging.

2. **ToU-aware smart charging** — shift 40% of sessions to WAPDA EV-3 off-peak rate (PKR 42/kWh) from on-peak (PKR 58/kWh). ~PKR 800K/year saving per site.

3. **OCPI 2.2 roaming** — connecting to the NTRC national hub (live 2027) adds 15–25% revenue from drivers on other networks, with zero additional capex.

**NTRC timeline Pakistan CPOs must plan for:**
• Now: Register with NTRC, file WAPDA EV-3 tariff application
• 2027: OCPP 2.0.1 TLS mandatory for new public fast chargers
• 2027: OCPI roaming mandatory for existing networks
• 2028: FBR digital invoice compliance for all CPOs above PKR 10M revenue
