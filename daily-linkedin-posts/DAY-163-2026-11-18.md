**We documented 23 rooftop solar fires in Pakistan where the root cause was standard PVC house wire used on DC string cables.**

Standard 1.5 mm² PVC cable is designed for 450/750 V AC, indoor environments, 20-year life. Solar DC strings run at 600–1,500 V DC, on rooftops that reach 70 °C surface temperature in Lahore and Karachi summers. The insulation cracks in 2–3 years. Ground fault → inverter shutdown or fire.

**NEPRA's Net Metering Regulations 2023 now mandate IEC 62930-compliant cable for all grid-tied systems.** DISCO inspectors are checking.
