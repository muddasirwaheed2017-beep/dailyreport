**What's inside the guide:**

🔬 **6 test types explained:**
Insulation Resistance (IR), Polarisation Index (PI), Dielectric Absorption Ratio (DAR), Step Voltage, HiPot (AC/DC/VLF), and Tan Delta — when to use each, and why a single IR reading at 60 seconds isn't enough for WAPDA T&C commissioning.

📊 **Test voltage selection matrix:**
Which voltage for which asset — 500V for LV motors, 2.5kV for 11kV transformers and cables, 5kV for 33kV/132kV (NTDC Grid Code 2023 mandatory), 10kV for EHV.

🇵🇰 **7 Pakistan-documented insulation failure modes:**

1. Monsoon moisture in transformer windings stored uncovered (18 DISCO failures in one cohort — all had PI < 1.5 at commissioning)
2. Load-shedding transient over-voltages cracking 11kV cable terminations (63% of 11kV cable failures — test each termination section independently)
3. Agricultural dust contamination of 33kV busbar insulators in Punjab's belt (34 of 47 substation bays below minimum IR)
4. Thin-film XLPE damage from excessive DC HiPot on aged cables (use VLF 0.1Hz instead — per NTDC TD-EE/2024-07)
5. Counterfeit meggers delivering sub-rated voltage (market survey 2024 — 8 of 22 instruments tested)
6. Temperature correction omission in summer — 3 Karachi transformer fires traced to this
7. Vacuum bottle integrity failures missed by IR-only testing on 11kV VCBs (11% of 200 refurbished VCBs — HiPot mandatory)

**One practical note on NTDC Grid Code 2023:**

Section 8.3.2 now requires *witnessed and submitted* IR/PI test reports before any 33kV or 132kV asset can be energised on the national grid. Three contractors received formal non-conformance notices in 2024–2025 for energising without proper documentation.

Your instrument needs to print or export a time-stamped PDF. A digital display isn't enough for NTDC submission anymore.
