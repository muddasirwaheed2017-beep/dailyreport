**Motor Efficiency Classes in Pakistan 2026 — What Every Industrial Buyer Needs to Know**

Pakistan's electricity tariffs have made motor efficiency class selection one of the highest-ROI decisions available to industrial buyers. Here's the core case:

**IE3 vs IE1 on a 37 kW motor:**
- Annual electricity saving: Rs 270,000 (at Rs 38/kWh blended, three-shift operation)
- IE3 motor cost premium: Rs 120,000–170,000 over IE1
- Simple payback: 6–8 months

For smaller motors, payback is even shorter. On a 7.5 kW motor, IE3 premium payback is typically 4–5 months at current LESCO/WAPDA B-2 tariffs.

**NEECA Regulatory Update:**
SRO 612(I)/2022 now mandates IE2 minimum for all three-phase motors 0.75–375 kW sold in regulated sectors. Customs authorities are increasingly checking IE class documentation at port. Procuring IE1 motors for manufacturing, textile, or water utility applications now carries import compliance risk in addition to higher operating costs.

**The four interventions — in order of ROI at Pakistani tariff:**
1. Correctly size the motor (42% of motors in Pakistani industry run below 50% load per NEECA 2024)
2. IE3 motor for continuous-duty ≥5.5 kW
3. VFD for variable-torque loads (pumps, fans, blowers)
4. Capacitor bank for PF penalty reduction (payback typically under 3 months)

**The rewinding decision:**
Pakistan has a large motor rewinding industry. For continuous-duty motors above 11 kW, a Tier-1 (EASA-affiliated) rewind maintains efficiency within 0.5%. Bazaar-grade rewinding causes 3–6% efficiency loss — at Pakistani tariffs, this costs Rs 60,000–180,000/year extra per motor, far exceeding the cost of the rewind itself.
