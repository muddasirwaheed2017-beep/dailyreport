**Why your UPS keeps switching to battery on generator supply — and 7 Pakistan-specific reasons it happens**

This is the most common industrial electrical complaint I hear from Pakistan's manufacturing sector: "We installed a generator for backup, but the UPS still goes to battery every time there's loadshedding."

The generator is running. The lights are on. But the UPS insists on drawing down the batteries.

Here's why — and the 7 failure modes unique to Pakistan's operating environment:

**1. Generator undersizing for non-linear loads**
The 1:1 rule (100 kVA generator for 100 kVA UPS) is wrong. Standard 6-pulse UPS rectifiers draw 250-350% inrush and require 2× minimum generator sizing. With Pakistan's 45°C summer ambient adding 10-15% derating, you need 2.3-2.5× in practice.

**2. AVR hunting from harmonic distortion**
Analogue AVRs on Chinese-brand generators interpret 5th harmonic current from UPS rectifiers as a regulation error and over-correct. This creates 2-5 Hz voltage oscillation that trips UPS input protection continuously.

**3. Synchronous reactance resonance (industrial zones)**
In LESCO/IESCO industrial zones where multiple facilities share a generator bus, passive harmonic filters resonate with generator Xs at 48 Hz (common during load acceptance). Result: filter capacitor failure within 6-12 months.

**4. PMG demagnetisation from high ambient**
Generator rooms reaching 65-75°C in Pakistan summer cause progressive PMG magnet demagnetisation — invisible in routine testing, but causing creeping voltage regulation degradation over 2-3 years.

**5. Governor droop miscalibration**
Mechanical governors have 3-5% frequency droop under load — beyond most UPS ±2 Hz input tolerance. Electronic isochronous governors fix this but are frequently skipped in Pakistani installations.

**6. Fuel contamination causing frequency instability**
Underground diesel storage in Pakistan's monsoon season leads to water contamination and microbial growth. Irregular fuel delivery causes ±3-5 Hz governor hunting — even on a "new" generator.

**7. Solar-generator-UPS interaction on bypass**
Pakistan's growing net-metering installations create a three-source synchronisation challenge. Solar inverter MPPT micro-frequency deviations (±0.1 Hz at 10-100 ms) desynchronise UPS bypass, causing hard switching that damages server PSUs and VFD capacitors.
