**Arc flash is Pakistan's most under-documented industrial electrocution hazard — and it's entirely preventable.**

Every year, electricians and engineers working on 11kV DISCO switchgear in Pakistan suffer fatal burns — not from electric shock, but from the explosive plasma and radiant heat of an uncontrolled arc flash. The physics are unforgiving: 0.05 seconds, 19,000°C, and it's over.

**The finding that shocked us most while writing this guide:**

Many LESCO and FESCO 11kV feeders have relay trip times of 0.5–1.2 seconds due to coordination margins. At 15 kA fault current and 0.5s clearing time, incident energy exceeds 65 cal/cm² — more than a Category 4 suit can handle alone. The engineering fix (faster relay setting or arc flash detection relay) costs a fraction of what one accident costs.

**The hierarchy of arc flash controls:**
1. De-energise and LOTO (always preferred)
2. Engineer to reduce incident energy (faster relay, AFD relay)
3. Increase working distance
4. PPE (last line of defence, not first response)
