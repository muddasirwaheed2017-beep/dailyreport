**7 facts that matter in Pakistan:**

**1. WAPDA voltage dips to 185V in summer.** Standard 220V±10% drivers cut out at 198V. Specify 176–264V input range as the minimum — or you'll face unexplained tripping all summer.

**2. Metal halide = 20 minutes dark after every load-shedding cut.** With 3–8 WAPDA cuts/day in summer, your factory floor is dark for 45–160 minutes daily. LED restarts instantly. This HSE argument alone justifies LED retrofit.

**3. Karachi coastal corrosion timeline: 6–9 months for IP20 fittings.** SITE, DHA, and Korangi coastal belt have 80–120 mg/m²/day chloride deposition. Minimum spec: IP66, ADC12 die-cast with Class 3 powder coat (60µm), conformal-coated driver PCB.

**4. Faisalabad textile weaving halls need 500 lux minimum.** Punjab Labor Department circular (2019) specifies 500 lux at working plane + UO≥0.6 + CRI≥80 for warp breakage inspection. Under-lit halls fail quality audits — and export buyers are noticing.

**5. PS 4778:2022 is now mandatory (SRO 991(I)/2022).** Imports without PSQCA certification attract PKR 50,000–500,000 fines per consignment. Check the PSQCA Product Certification Mark before any bulk order.

**6. 45°C ambient temperature halves driver lifespan.** Most LED datasheets spec L70 at Ta=25°C or Ta=40°C. At Pakistani rooftop temperatures of 45–52°C in summer, L70 drops from 50,000h to 30,000–35,000h. Always request the driver derating curve and verify L70 at Ta=50°C.

**7. Halal food processing: flicker index <0.1 required.** Camera-based conveyor inspection lines need IEEE PAR 1789-2015 flicker-free drivers. NEPRA's 230V±10% supply variation affects PWM frequencies — standard drivers fail camera QC lines.
