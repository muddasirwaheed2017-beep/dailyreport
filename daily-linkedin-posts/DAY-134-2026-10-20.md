## Chain Drive & Sprocket Pakistan 2026

---

**A Faisalabad flour mill is replacing their ANSI #50 roller chain every 8 months. They should be getting 4+ years.**

What's going wrong? Three interconnected Pakistan-specific failures:

**1. Wrong service factor**
Standard ANSI service factor: 1.3 (uniform load, chain drive)
With 6 DOL load-shedding restarts per day: 1.3 + 0.25 = 1.55
Required design power: 5 kW × 1.55 = 7.75 kW → should be on #60 chain, not #50

**2. Lubrication varnishing in May–September**
At 45°C ambient, the drive reaches 85°C chain temperature.
ISO VG 46 oil (commonly used) oxidises to black varnish above 80°C.
That varnish increases wear — it's abrasive, not lubricating.
Solution: ISO VG 100–150 with anti-oxidant additive in summer months.

**3. Running new chain on worn sprocket**
Sprocket teeth are hooked (shark-fin profile) from years of wear.
The new chain's rollers ride the hook — immediate accelerated wear.
Replace chain + sprocket together when sprocket teeth are visibly hooked.

Pakistan's roller chain failure rate is almost entirely preventable with three adjustments:
→ Correct service factor for DOL restarts
→ Summer lubrication viscosity upgrade
→ Chain + sprocket replacement together
