## Day 89 LinkedIn Post — 2026-09-05

**Headline:**
NEPRA NEM 3.0 Changed Solar Economics in Pakistan — Here's the Data (2026 Buyer's Perspective)

**Body:**
Pakistan's solar net metering framework fundamentally changed in 2023. Many buyers are still being sold systems sized for the old NEM 2.0 rules (1:1 unit banking). Under NEM 3.0 Net Billing, the math is very different.

**The core shift:**
→ Import from WAPDA: PKR 45–62/kWh (peak, UR-1 high slabs)
→ Export to WAPDA: PKR 10.69/kWh (NEPRA avoided-cost rate)
→ Self-consumption multiplier: 4.2–5.8× more valuable than exporting

**What this means for system sizing:**
A 10kW system on a 400-unit/month household bill will export ~50% of generation. Under NEM 2.0, that export earned the same as avoided import. Under Net Billing, it earns 5× less. The financially optimal system under Net Billing is sized to your daytime (09:00–17:00) load — not your annual bill.

**Per-DISCO realities (2026):**
• LESCO (Lahore): 6–10 week processing, good bi-dir meter availability
• IESCO (Islamabad): 8–14 weeks, moderate availability
• PESCO (Peshawar): 16–26 weeks ⚠️, significant backlog
• SEPCO (Sukkur/Larkana): 16–30 weeks ⚠️, very limited bi-dir meters
• K-Electric (Karachi): 8–14 weeks, own meter rollout active

**Technical checks buyers skip:**
1. Feeder capacity check BEFORE purchasing equipment (if feeder is full, application is rejected regardless of system quality)
2. IEC 62116 type-approval certificate for the inverter (NEPRA requires this, not UL 1741)
3. Earthing resistance test at commissioning (> 5Ω = failed inspection = delayed commissioning)

**Battery storage under Net Billing:**
A 5kWh LiFePO4 battery shifting 4kWh/day from export to evening self-consumption adds PKR 49,000–74,000/year in value (at PKR 38–50/kWh displacement vs PKR 10.69/kWh export). Over a 7-year battery cycle life, this contributes PKR 350,000–520,000 — often more than the panels themselves under NEM 3.0.
