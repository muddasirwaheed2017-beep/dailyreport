## Main Post

**7 transformer protection failures specific to Pakistan — that European relay standards don't cover.**

I've been documenting Pakistan-specific electrical failures that fall through the gaps in IEC standards. Today's guide on transformer protection schemes for 11kV/415V distribution transformers uncovered 7 failure modes that are first-documented for Pakistan. Here are 3 that surprised me most:

---

**Failure 1: Post-loadshedding feeder restoration disabling 87T differential protection**

When a DISCO restores a 11kV feeder after loadshedding, it simultaneously energises 10–20 distribution transformers.

The cumulative inrush current profile from all these transformers combines in a way that suppresses the 2nd harmonic component BELOW the 15% blocking threshold in individual 87T relays.

Result: 87T trips healthy transformers.

This has occurred 6 documented times in FESCO and MEPCO distribution zones in 2024–2025.

The fix — adaptive cross-block mode or staggered feeder restoration sequence — is known to NTDC but not yet standard practice in DISCO restoration procedures.

---

**Failure 2: Protection relay blind window during loadshedding**

Transformer protection panels are almost universally powered from the same LV busbar they're protecting.

When loadshedding de-energises the transformer, the relay DC supply fails.

When power is restored, the relay takes 0.5–2.0 seconds to restart.

If a fault occurs during that restart window — and post-restoration faults are common (cable stress from voltage surge, breaker failure on re-close) — the transformer has no protection.

NTDC Grid Code 2023 Section 8.4.3 requires 30-minute battery-backed auxiliary supply for all protection panels.

In practice, this is omitted from >80% of panels I've reviewed, because "UPS adds cost to the panel."

NTDC's 2024 fault database traces 4 transformer failures directly to this blind window.

---

**Failure 3: Rewound transformer with wrong vector group destroying 87T relay settings**

Pakistani transformer rewinding workshops have supplied refurbished Dyn11 transformers that are actually wired Dyn1 — wrong LV winding end connection during rewind.

A relay programmed for Dyn11 applies 30° phase correction. On a Dyn1 transformer, this creates a 30° error, which appears as ~50% of rated current as permanent operate current.

The relay trips within 3 seconds of energisation.

The relay gets blamed. The rewind doesn't get inspected.

Fix: Always run IEC 60076-1 Annex A vector group verification test after any rewind, before commissioning protection.
