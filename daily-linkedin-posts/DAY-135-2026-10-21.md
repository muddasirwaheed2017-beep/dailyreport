**3 ball screw mistakes costing Pakistan machine shops thousands in premature failures — and how to fix them**

Pakistan's CNC retrofit market is growing fast, particularly in Sialkot, Gujranwala, and Lahore. But 60–70% of ball screw failures I see in Pakistani workshops come from the same three errors:

**Mistake 1: Wrong grease for Pakistan summer**
Standard automotive grease oxidises at 80°C into abrasive grit. Punjab summer: ball screw temperatures routinely hit 90–105°C in direct-sun installations. Fix: Mobilux EP 2 or SKF LGEP 2, rated to 130°C. Regreasing interval in dusty mills: halve the standard recommendation.

**Mistake 2: No load-shedding restart factor in force calculations**
Standard force calculation assumes clean motor start. WAPDA/LESCO load shedding means DOL restarts multiple times daily, creating 4–6× rated torque spikes in 1–3 seconds. For Pakistani actuator sizing, multiply acceleration force component by 2.5× to avoid ball screw preload damage.

**Mistake 3: Counterfeit ball screws from Pakistan market**
Fake THK/Hiwin labels are widespread. Genuine surface hardness: 60–62 HRC. Fake: often 52–55 HRC — fails in 500–2,000 km vs 50,000+ km for genuine. Quick field test: weigh the screw (fake Ø20×1000mm weighs 700–780g; genuine weighs 850–920g) and wipe markings with acetone (genuine laser-etched markings survive; fake pad prints smear).
