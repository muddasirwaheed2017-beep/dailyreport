## V-Belt, Timing Belt & Pulley Drive Pakistan 2026

---

**Why Pakistan factories lose PKR 280,000+ per year on one V-belt drive — and the 10-minute fix**

In a Faisalabad textile mill last year, a maintenance team replaced the same three B-section V-belts on a 37 kW centrifugal fan every 3–4 months.

The root cause wasn't belt quality. It was two things:
1. The belts were over-tensioned by the installer (thumb-pressure method — feels right, actually applies 50–80 N on a belt rated for 35–55 N max)
2. The motor base was twisted 0.8 mm, creating 1.6 mm pulley offset at the face

The NEECA energy audit calculation on that same drive: at 5% efficiency loss (mis-tensioned, misaligned drive), running 16 hours/day × 300 days/year on HTSC-A tariff (PKR ~32/kWh) = **PKR 284,000/year wasted on one drive**.

The deflection-force tensioning fix:
- Free span 700 mm → deflection target = 700 × 1.6% = 11.2 mm
- B-section reference force = 35–55 N (about 3.5–5.5 kg weight applied at midpoint)
- Takes 10 minutes. Requires a ruler and a spring balance.
