**Selecting the right HMI for Pakistani industrial environments in 2026 — a practical checklist**

**1. WAPDA brownout protection is non-negotiable**
Grid drops to 185 VAC urban / 160 VAC rural in summer. Use 24 VDC DIN-rail SMPS (85–265 VAC wide-range input) + battery backup. Rs 10–15K investment prevents losses worth far more.

**2. IP65 front panel as the minimum**
Cotton fly, bagasse dust, cement dust. IP65 front bezel is table stakes. Specify conformal-coated PCBs for Karachi coastal sites.

**3. 7-inch widescreen = Pakistan sweet spot**
Fits DIN-86 cutouts. Rs 45K–130K covers 80% of MCC and pump station applications. Multi-protocol (MODBUS RTU/TCP + EtherNet/IP) handles essentially every Pakistani installation.

**4. Budget for engineering, not just hardware**
30–40% hardware, 60–70% engineering. A Rs 75K HMI for a 48-feeder MCC needs Rs 50–80K engineering. Get integrator references.

**5. MODBUS RTU wiring rules still getting broken**
— 120-ohm termination at both ends
— Shielded twisted-pair (Belden 9841 equiv, available Lahore)
— 200mm separation from ≥48VAC cables
— TVS diodes on RS-485 terminals

**6. Same-brand HMI+PLC = 40–60% faster commissioning**
Tag database imports directly. Dramatically reduces commissioning time vs cross-brand MODBUS mapping.
