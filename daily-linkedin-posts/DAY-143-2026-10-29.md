**The 3 Most Expensive HMI Mistakes Pakistan Panel Builders Make**

We've seen all three of these cause real problems on Pakistan sites in 2026.

**Mistake 1: Putting HMI and VFDs on the same RS-485 Modbus bus**

When the HMI polls the PLC AND tries to also poll the VFDs, bus collisions happen. The symptom: intermittent communication losses that mysteriously disappear when you're on site and return at 2 AM. The fix is a separate RS-485 bus for HMI↔PLC and another for PLC↔VFDs.

**Mistake 2: No 24VDC UPS on the HMI supply**

WAPDA's 80–200ms voltage sag before a full outage reboots the HMI. The operator loses their unsaved recipe setpoints. On the next WAPDA restoration, the machine starts with default values. A DIN-rail 24V UPS module costs PKR 8,500–22,000 — far less than one production batch lost to wrong setpoints.

**Mistake 3: Specifying capacitive touch for production environments**

Operators in Pakistani textile mills, cold storage, and food plants wear gloves. Capacitive touchscreens need bare fingers. The result: frustrated operators pressing harder and harder, ultimately using a screwdriver or key as a stylus — which destroys the screen. Always specify resistive touch for any production environment.
