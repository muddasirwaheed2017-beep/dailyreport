**🔴 Why does your HRC fuse blow every summer in Pakistan — and how to stop it**

We get this call every June from panel builders across Multan, Sukkur, and Faisalabad:

*"Our HRC fuse keeps tripping at this one feeder — we've replaced it three times. The panel is fine in winter."*

The answer is almost always **ambient temperature derating** — and it's one of the most overlooked factors in Pakistani switchgear specification.

---

**Here's what's happening:**

HRC fuses (BS 88, DIN NH, cylindrical) are calibrated and rated at **20°C ambient**. IEC 60269 requires derating as temperature rises:

| Ambient °C | Derating Factor | 100A fuse carries |
|---|---|---|
| 20°C | 1.00 | 100A |
| 35°C | 0.90 | 90A (Lahore spring) |
| 40°C | 0.85 | 85A (Islamabad summer) |
| 45°C | 0.80 | **80A** (Multan, Sukkur, Hyderabad) |
| 50°C | 0.75 | 75A (Jacobabad peak summer) |

A panel room at 45°C with an 85A feeder and a 100A fuse is sitting at **106% of the derated fuse capacity**. It will operate in summer. Every year.

---

**The fix:** Upsize the fuse carrier by one standard step at the design stage.

85A feeder in Multan → specify 125A fuse carrier (not 100A). Verify the cable is rated for 125A. Done.
