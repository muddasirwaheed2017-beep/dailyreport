**The PKR 12,000 device that prevents PKR 3.2M losses — and why 78% of Pakistani industrial panels don't have it.**

Surge Protective Devices (SPDs) are the most systematically under-specified protection device in Pakistani industrial panels. A 2023 FESCO zone survey found 78% of 214 industrial facilities had no Type 1 SPD installed — despite Pakistan's monsoon lightning ground flash density of 8–60 GFD/km²/year.

Three Pakistan-specific specification errors I see repeatedly:

**1. Using 255V Uc on WAPDA networks**
IEC 61643-11's standard 1.1 × Un = 253V recommendation is based on European 230V grids. WAPDA LV networks in Lahore, Faisalabad, and Multan run 260–280V in June–September. A 255V Uc SPD reaches thermal runaway within 6–8 months. Correct spec: 320V Uc (L-N) minimum for all WAPDA DISCO zones.

**2. Installing Type 2 SPD only at the MDB from overhead line**
Type 2 handles induced surges (8/20 µs waveform — In rating). Direct and near-strike lightning on WAPDA overhead lines delivers a 10/350 µs impulse — the Iimp test. A Type 2 SPD cannot absorb this. Type 1 (Iimp rated) is mandatory at every overhead LV service entry in Pakistan.

**3. Wrong protection mode for Karachi (K-Electric TT earthing)**
WAPDA DISCOs use TN-S earthing. K-Electric uses TT. A Mode 1 (3-pole, L-N) SPD installed in Karachi misses the N-PE discharge path and loses 40–60% of its surge current capability. Mode 3 (3+1 pole) or Mode 4 (4-pole) is mandatory in the TT zone.

**The 2026 baseline specification for any new Pakistani industrial panel:**
- Type 1+2 combination at MDB (Iimp 25 kA, In 40 kA, Mode 4, Uc 440V L-L)
- Type 2 at every sub-DB (In 20 kA, 4-pole, Mode 4, remote fault signalling)
- Type 3 at all PLCs, VFDs, and SCADA panels
