## Lightning Protection System (LPS) IEC 62305 Pakistan — 7 Things Your LPS Contractor Won't Tell You

---

**7 things about Lightning Protection Systems in Pakistan that no contractor will tell you:**

Pakistan's NBC 2021 Chapter 18 now mandates IEC 62305-2 risk assessment for every building above 15 metres. LDA and CDA began enforcing this at Completion Certificate stage in Q4 2024. PEC-licensed electrical engineers are personally liable. Yet most LPS installations in Pakistan today are non-compliant.

Here's what's actually going on:

**1. Pakistan's Ng values are 2–4× higher than IEC 62305 European defaults**
Islamabad/Murree: Ng = 6.0–9.0 flashes/km²/year. Lahore: 3.5–5.0. Most LPS designs in Pakistan use European Ng values from the standard itself — drastically underestimating strike probability. Use PMD isokeraunic data, not IEC Annex A.

**2. ESE rods (PKR 120,000–250,000) are not IEC 62305 compliant**
IEC 62305-1 Section 4.3 explicitly states ESE devices lack scientific validation and are not recognised. LDA and CDA do not accept NFC 17-102 ESE certificates for NBC 2021-mandated buildings. A correctly designed Franklin rod mesh system costs 80–90% less and is fully compliant.

**3. Your rooftop GRP water tank creates a side-flash hazard**
GRP (fibreglass) tanks are non-conductive — they cannot be bonded to the LPS. But their metallic fittings must be bonded. Any person standing within 1 m of an unbonded GRP tank fitting during a strike faces a lethal arcing risk. Not documented anywhere else on Pakistani e-commerce.

**4. Rooftop DG sets need an HF spark gap bond — not a direct earth connection**
Pakistan's load-shedding DG sets: if you directly bond the generator frame to the LPS earth, a direct strike sends 10–100 kA through the generator exhaust into the diesel fuel system. IEC 62305-3 Table E.1 requires a 1.5 kV high-frequency spark gap bond between DG PE and LPS earth to prevent fire/explosion.

**5. BTS towers on your rooftop need equipotential bonding**
Telecom operators install a separate ≤2Ω technical earth isolated from the building's main earth. This violates IEC 62305-3 Clause 5.3 and can generate 50–200 kV between the two earth systems during a strike — destroying the BTS and damaging the building. All earth systems must be bonded at a single main equipotential bar.

**6. Measure earth resistance in December, not August**
Pakistan's monsoon season temporarily reduces soil resistance by 30–60%. Commissioning measurements in August look great — December measurements may reveal a non-compliant system. IEC 62305-3 requires dry-season verification.

**7. NBC 2021 enforcement is real and accelerating**
CDA commenced inspections Q1 2025. LDA Q4 2024. Any building permit application for >15m construction now requires LPS design drawings with IEC 62305-2 RA at plan approval stage, and an earth resistance test certificate (≤10Ω for Class III) at Completion Certificate.
