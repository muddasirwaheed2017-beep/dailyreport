**Post:**

LESCO's 2024 audit of 480 commercial net-metered solar systems in Pakistan found 67 cases where the customer's inverter cloud log and the DISCO meter reading differed by more than 5%.

In 44 of those cases, the customer had no independent datalogger.

Result: net-metering payments withheld pending reconciliation.

Here's what most solar installers don't tell their customers in Pakistan:

**Your inverter WiFi app is not NEPRA-compliant evidence for 50kW+ systems.**

Under TSDG 2023, any commercial system above 50kW must have:
- An IEC 61724-1 compliant datalogger
- 15-minute interval export logging
- 10-year data retention

The 3 most common monitoring failures we see in the field:
1. RS485 without termination resistors → Modbus dropout on multi-inverter plants (61% of communication faults)
2. Logger powered from main supply → data gap every load shedding cycle
3. Generic inverter with no cloud server after 12 months → permanent data gap
