**Types:**
• DOL Contactor Panel (1–10T, budget-friendly)
• Resistance-Step Starter (slip-ring motors, heavy cranes)
• VFD-Based Panel (modern standard — precise speed, anti-sway, 30–45% WAPDA savings)
• PLC Smart Panel (automated warehouses, ISO 45001 compliance)

**Key specification points Pakistani buyers miss:**
1. Phase-loss relay is MANDATORY — not optional. WAPDA single-phasing destroys crane motors in minutes.
2. Electronic overload relays (not bimetal) for cranes with >6 starts/hour.
3. VFD brake coordination — brake release delay + DC injection timing. The #1 failure point on VFD crane installations in Pakistan.
4. Master-follower synchronisation for double-girder cranes with two long-travel motors.
5. Bilingual Urdu/English safety labels required by PSQCA + Factories Act 1934 — non-negotiable.
