**Why does your 11kV VCB panel trip when you don't want it to — and NOT trip when you do?**

The answer is almost always one of these three protection relay commissioning failures:

**1. Wrong IDMT curve type**
Pakistani DISCOs use IEC 60255-151 Standard Inverse (SI) on most 11kV feeders. If your relay is set to Extremely Inverse (EI), your discrimination margins collapse — the upstream breaker trips before yours. K-Electric commonly specifies Very Inverse (VI) for cable feeders. Use the wrong curve = nuisance tripping or failure to discriminate.

**2. CT class mismatch**
Using a measuring-class CT (0.2S, 0.5, 1, 3) instead of a protection-class CT (5P20, PS). Measuring CTs saturate above 1.5–3× rated current. Under an 11kV fault condition (typically 6–15× rated), the CT output distorts and the relay either fails to operate in time or misoperates entirely. This is a life-safety failure, not just an equipment issue.

**3. No DISCO settings approval before energization**
In Pakistan, every DISCO (LESCO, K-Electric, FESCO, IESCO) issues a Protection Relay Settings Letter specifying Plug Setting (PS%), Time Multiplier Setting (TMS), and Instantaneous Setting. Installing your own settings without DISCO approval is a violation — the DISCO can require complete de-energization and protection redesign.
