**8 things most MDB quotations in Pakistan omit:**

1. **45°C busbar derating** — A "1,600A" panel only safely carries 1,408A at 45°C (Pakistan summer standard). Specify busbars at 125% rated current.

2. **DISCO fault level by zone** — LESCO/K-Electric sites see 50kA fault current. A 36kA ACB here is a safety violation waiting to happen.

3. **Form of separation** — WAPDA metering bays require Form 4b. Insisting on Form 2 is a DISCO connection rejection risk.

4. **WAPDA metering CT class by tariff** — C2 industrial tariff needs 0.5S class CT, not standard 0.5. Wrong class = billing inaccuracy = disputes.

5. **Aluminium vs copper busbar** — Aluminium saves 60–70% material cost for 1,000A+ MDBs but requires bi-metallic clamps and is unsuitable within 5km of Karachi coastline.

6. **Under-voltage protection setting** — Standard IEC setting trips during WAPDA restoration transients. Pakistan-specific: 85% Vn with 2-second delay.

7. **Incomer device sizing** — A 1,000kVA transformer needs an ACB rated 2,000A (not 1,600A) after 45°C derating — most quotations omit this.

8. **SPD at MDB** — Type 1+2, Iimp ≥ 12.5kA for overhead WAPDA LT lines (Lahore, Faisalabad, Multan, rural zones).
