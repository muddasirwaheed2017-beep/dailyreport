**Three things Pakistan's EV installers consistently get wrong:**

1. **Wrong RCD type** — Type A RCCB does NOT protect against DC smooth leakage from modern EV onboard chargers. Type B is mandatory under PSQCA PS:5201:2023. Using Type A is both a safety risk and a nuisance-tripping source.

2. **Shared circuits** — EV chargers draw near-rated current continuously for 1–8 hours. Never put them on a shared ring circuit. A dedicated 32A MCB (Type C, not Type B) per EVSE is required.

3. **OCPP 1.6J for public chargers** — It's cheaper but won't support NTRC's forthcoming 2027 smart charging mandate or ISO 15118 Plug & Charge. Specify OCPP 2.0.1 for any public CPO deployment today.

**The LESCO demand charge trap for CPOs:**

A 60kW DC charger nominally adds 60 kVA to your monthly demand — PKR 51,000/month fixed capacity charge even if used only 2 hours/day. Smart charging profiles via OCPP 2.0.1 DLM (Dynamic Load Management) can stagger activation and cut this demand by 30–40%.

**Solar + EV charging — the Pakistani residential use case:**

10kWh LiFePO4 battery → hybrid inverter → 7kW EVSE = full load-shedding resilience for home EV charging. 30–40 km range top-up from yesterday's solar generation, no WAPDA required. Break-even on solar-EV system: typically 2.5–3.5 years at current electricity and petrol prices.
