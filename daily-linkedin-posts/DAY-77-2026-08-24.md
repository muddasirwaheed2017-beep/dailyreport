**🏭 SCADA in Pakistan 2026: NEPRA Mandate, Pricing in PKR, and 8 Specs Most Suppliers Miss**

As of 1 January 2026, NEPRA requires every captive power plant above 1 MW in Pakistan to operate a real-time SCADA system reporting 15-minute generation data to the relevant DISCO. The penalty for non-compliance: PKR 500,000 per month under NEPRA Conduct of Business Rules 2022 (Rule 47).

If your plant is in that bracket and you haven't yet specified your SCADA system, here is the practical procurement picture for 2026.

**8 Pakistan-specific specs most SCADA suppliers won't mention:**

1. **WAPDA brownout power**: RTU supply must tolerate 185–270 VAC or 9–30 VDC (UPS/solar hybrid), not a standard 220 VAC ±10% device.

2. **NEPRA protocol**: New captive plants must support IEC 60870-5-104 (TCP/IP) for DISCO data feed — MODBUS-only SCADA will not satisfy the audit interface requirement.

3. **Cement mill conformal coating**: Calcium carbonate dust bridges PCB tracks at 85% RH. All RTUs and field I/O in cement environments must specify IEC 60068-2-11 conformal coat (acrylic or polyurethane class AR/UR) from the manufacturer. Always request the CoC.

4. **Sugar mill islanding speed**: Grid-loss islanding sequence must complete in <2 seconds. PC-based SCADA with 1–2 second Ethernet scan cycles cannot achieve this — specify PLCs with hardware interrupt DI for the grid-loss signal.

5. **WASA Lahore 4G LTE + WhatsApp**: WASA alarm integration uses WhatsApp Business API as a supplement only. NEPRA auditors do not accept WhatsApp as the primary alarm log — a historian is mandatory.

6. **Punjab PIPIP drip-irrigation RTUs**: Spec 9–30 VDC wide-range power input, 4G primary/GPRS fallback, dual-SIM for coverage gaps. Budget PKR 40,000–70,000 per remote node all-in.

7. **Telenor/Jazz 4G gaps**: Cholistan and upper Balochistan have 4G dead zones. Specify dual-SIM routers for any remote site outside major urban and agricultural corridors.

8. **Customs duty savings**: SCADA RTUs under HS 8537.10 attract 11% customs duty + 17% GST standard. DTRE-registered exporters and EDB-approved projects can claim nil duty under SRO 450(I)/2001. File before your import order — approval takes 2–4 weeks but saves real money on large projects.

**NEPRA SCADA Compliance Checklist (7 items):**

✅ 15-minute interval generation data (kWh import, export, net generation)
✅ Power quality data: 3-phase voltage, frequency, power factor
✅ Secure remote access for DISCO auditors (VPN + OPC UA or IEC 60870-5-104)
✅ 12-month rolling historian with tamper-evident timestamps
✅ IEC 62443-2-1 cybersecurity: unique user accounts, no default credentials, audit log
✅ Redundant communication (primary + fallback)
✅ UPS-backed server runtime >4 hours
