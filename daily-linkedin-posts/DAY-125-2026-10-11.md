**Industrial Electrical Installation Standards in Pakistan 2026 — What Every Consultant and Contractor Must Know**

Pakistan's electrical installation regulatory framework has multiple layers that often trip up even experienced engineers. Here's a quick orientation:

**The Regulatory Stack:**
- NEPRA Grid Code 2023 → Distribution Code → Metering Code (national grid layer)
- PSQCA PS 3646 (based on IEC 60364) → national wiring code (internal installation layer)
- DISCO Connection Policy (LESCO, K-Electric, FESCO, etc.) → service entry layer
- Provincial Electricity Inspectorate → inspection and certification layer

**Key Technical Points:**

1. **Cable sizing correction:** IEC 60364-5-52 current-capacity tables assume 30°C ambient. Pakistani summer routinely hits 45–50°C. Apply correction factor Ct = 0.87 (40°C) to 0.75 (50°C). This often means going up 1–2 cross-section steps from the European-calculated size.

2. **System type depends on your DISCO:**
   - Urban LESCO/FESCO/IESCO supply → TN-C-S (PEN splits at Main Earthing Terminal)
   - Rural MEPCO/HESCO/QESCO → often TT system (consumer earth electrode + RCD mandatory)
   - Large industrial/sensitive loads → TN-S preferred (separate N and PE throughout)

3. **PMT (Pole-Mounted Transformer) LV connection:** The 4-core aluminium XLPE armoured cable from the PMT to the consumer service head must be sized correctly: 100 kVA PMT → 139 A FLC → minimum 95 mm² Al XLPE.

4. **LESCO energisation requires an EI Certificate (Form EI-2):** You need a PEC-licensed electrical contractor to complete testing (IR test ≥1 MΩ, earth continuity ≤0.5 Ω, RCD trip ≤40 ms), then a provincial Electrical Inspector to witness and sign off. Only then will LESCO energise.

5. **Industrial socket standard:** IEC 60309 (CEE form) 32A/63A 3P+N+E IP44 — not BS 1363 domestic plugs. Many Pakistani industrial sites are wired incorrectly with domestic sockets on 3-phase circuits.
