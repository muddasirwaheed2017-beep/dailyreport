**What's driving demand in 2026?**

Three regulatory drivers are creating hard, non-deferrable procurement demand:

**1. NEECA SRO 1065-I/2023 — Boiler Combustion Efficiency**
Every boiler above 500 kg/h steam output must now submit quarterly flue gas analysis reports. The target: O₂ 2–4%, CO below 100 ppm, stack temperature below 200°C for NEECA compliance. A portable combustion analyser costs PKR 120,000–350,000. The compliance penalty and mandatory audit costs far more.

**2. OGRA Gas Detection Directive 2022 — CNG Stations**
All 3,500+ CNG stations must have fixed methane detection with Level 2 auto-shutdown at 25% LEL. NDIR infrared sensors are now the technical preference over catalytic bead — they resist poisoning from H₂S traces in Pakistan's sour gas network and outlast pellistor sensors by 3–4 years.

**3. NEQ 2000 / SEPA/PEPA ETP Compliance**
Industrial effluent discharge requires continuous pH monitoring (6–10 range). SEPA inspection teams are now checking for calibrated online pH analyzers as a compliance condition, not just spot-test pH meters.

**Technology Snapshot: Which sensor for which application?**

| Technology | Best for | Avoid if |
|---|---|---|
| Electrochemical (EC) | H₂S, CO, O₂, Cl₂, NH₃ at ppm level | High H₂S + CO simultaneously (cross-poisoning) |
| Catalytic bead (pellistor) | Low-cost CH₄/LPG LEL | H₂S-contaminated gas streams |
| NDIR infrared | CH₄, CO₂, CO — poison-resistant | Budget-limited installations |
| PID | VOCs, benzene, toluene in pharma/paint | CH₄, CO, H₂S (no PID response) |
| Ultrasonic UGLD | High-pressure gas leak detection | Low-pressure releases; noisy environments |

---

**ATEX matters in Pakistan now.**

OGRA inspection teams are increasingly checking IECEx certificates on gas detection equipment during refinery and CNG station audits. CE marking alone is not sufficient for Zone 1 installations. If your fixed detector doesn't carry ATEX/IECEx certification and you're in a Zone 1 classified area — oil pump seals, CNG compressor halls, H₂S scrubber outlets — you have a compliance gap that needs to close before the next OGRA inspection.
