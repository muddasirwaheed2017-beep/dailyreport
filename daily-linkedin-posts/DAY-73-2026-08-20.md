# Topic: Why Pakistan UPS Batteries Die in 12–18 Months (And How to Stop It)

---

**Why Pakistani UPS batteries die at 12–18 months — and how to fix it**

I see this question almost every week from Pakistan's electrical buyers:

*"We bought a quality UPS, good brand, genuine battery. It died in 14 months. Why?"*

The answer isn't the battery. It's the room.

---

**The Arrhenius Problem**

VRLA (sealed lead-acid) batteries are rated at 25°C. At 25°C, a good AGM battery delivers 3–5 years of service.

But here's the Arrhenius law applied to batteries:

Every 10°C rise in ambient temperature **halves** battery life.

At 35°C → 1.5–2.5 years
At 40°C → 1.0–1.5 years
At 45°C → 9–12 months ← *where Pakistan lands in summer*
At 50°C → 6–8 months ← *unventilated electrical rooms*

Most Pakistan electrical rooms in summer run 42–52°C. No AC. Poor ventilation. Direct sun on the building roof.

The battery isn't failing. It's being cooked.

---

**The Fix: Three Steps**

**Step 1 — Temperature control is the single highest-ROI investment**
A 1-ton split AC unit for the UPS room costs PKR 80,000–120,000. A set of 4–8 replacement VRLA batteries costs PKR 60,000–200,000 every 12–18 months. The AC pays back in under a year and your batteries now last 4+ years.

**Step 2 — Wide input range, not narrow**
A UPS with a narrow input range (195–240 V) switches to battery every time Lahore/Faisalabad mains dips below 195 V at night. That can be 20–30 battery cycles per day. Every cycle ages the battery. A line-interactive UPS with AVR (170–270 V range) absorbs those swings without ever touching the battery.

**Step 3 — Generator mode selection**
Online UPS units without generator mode configured will go to battery during generator operation because the generator THDv (8–12%) exceeds the UPS input acceptance. More unnecessary cycling. Configure generator mode or install an isolation transformer between generator output and UPS input.

---

**The LiFePO4 Alternative**

For Pakistan buyers who've accepted the reality of poor infrastructure and want a decade-long solution: LiFePO4 battery UPS.

- 3,000–5,000 cycle life vs 200–500 for VRLA
- Operates at 45°C without dramatic life reduction
- 50% weight and space reduction
- 3–4× higher initial cost, but payback in 4–6 years in Pakistan market

We're seeing early adopters in Karachi data centres and Lahore medical facilities making the switch in 2025–26.

---

**The Pakistan UPS Specification Checklist**

Beyond temperature: always check these for Pakistan sites:
✅ Input range 170–270 V minimum (160–280 V for rural feeders)
✅ Pure sine wave output (not modified sine — kills active PFC PSUs)
✅ Generator compatible (generator mode, input THD tolerance ≥15%)
✅ Temperature-compensated charging
✅ Manual maintenance bypass (mandatory for any UPS >3 KVA)
✅ SNMP for server room / data centre integration

For solar hybrid UPS: verify AEDB/NEPRA net metering approval before connecting to grid.
