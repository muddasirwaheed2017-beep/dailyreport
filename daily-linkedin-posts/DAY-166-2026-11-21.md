**The hidden cost of EV fleet charging in Pakistan — and how to cut it by 54%**

As Pakistan accelerates its EV transition — NTRC mandate, NEPRA EV tariffs, BYD/Honda/Suzuki EVs in market — fleet operators are discovering that the charger hardware is the easy part. The real complexity is the electricity bill.

Here's a worked example from our latest buyer guide:

**30-bus EV fleet depot, Lahore (LESCO EV-3 tariff)**

Without smart charging:
- 30 × 22 kW chargers plug in simultaneously at 20:00
- Peak demand: 660 kW
- Sanctioned load needed: 700 kW
- LESCO demand charge: PKR 595,000/month (fixed!)
- Total monthly electricity cost: PKR 661,000

With OCPP 2.0.1 Dynamic Load Management:
- Staggered charging across 11-hour window
- Peak demand capped: 245 kW
- Sanctioned load: 280 kW
- LESCO demand charge: PKR 238,000/month
- Total monthly electricity cost: PKR 304,000

**Monthly saving: PKR 357,000 (54% reduction)**
**OCPP DLM system cost: PKR 450,000**
**Payback: 1.3 months**
