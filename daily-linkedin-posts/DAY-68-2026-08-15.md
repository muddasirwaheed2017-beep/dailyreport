**847 electrocution deaths in Pakistan in FY2024–25 (NEPRA). Earthing failure is the #1 cause.**

After field surveys across Lahore, Karachi, Faisalabad, Multan, Islamabad, Gujranwala, Hyderabad and Peshawar, here are the 3 costliest earthing pit failures we see repeatedly:

---

**1. The 1-Bag Bentonite Error — Pakistan's Most Expensive Rs. 400 Saving**

A standard commercial earthing pit (600mm × 600mm × 3m) requires 8–9 bags (50kg each) of sodium bentonite — approximately Rs. 112,000–189,000 in compound alone.

Pakistani contractors typically use 1–2 bags.

Result: commissioning resistance of 5Ω becomes 38Ω by the following dry season. The RCCB requires &lt;2Ω to function correctly. At 38Ω, a 5.1mA fault flows — the RCD never trips.

The exposed metalwork stays live.

The re-excavation, re-treatment, and re-commissioning cost: Rs. 35,000–85,000. Plus the liability.

---

**2. Black Cotton Soil Rod Migration — 4.2 Million Hectares Affected**

Multan, Bahawalpur, Rahimyar Khan, Dera Ghazi Khan — black cotton soil (Vertisol) undergoes 15–30% volumetric swelling in monsoon and contracts in dry season.

The cyclic pressure migrates under-filled earth rods upward by 8–14cm per season.

After 3–4 cycles: the connection emerges from the ground.

Fix: anti-migration clamp at 300mm depth + full bentonite fill + 48-hour cure before backfill. Rs. 850 in materials. Preventing Rs. 40,000–80,000 reinstallation cost.

---

**3. NEPRA Net Metering Lockable Cover — Non-Compliance Rate Estimated >60%**

NEPRA's 2024 distributed generation inspection protocol (applied from 2024-07-01 across all DISCOs) requires a lockable IP55-rated inspection cover with labelling "EARTHING TEST POINT" visible throughout the 25-year connection period.

We estimate &gt;60% of net metering installations completed before 2024-07-01 are non-compliant.

Rectification cost: Rs. 8,000–18,000 per site (cover replacement + labelling + re-inspection fee). Much less than a connection suspension.
