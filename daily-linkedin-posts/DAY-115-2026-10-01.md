**7 differentiators for correct Pakistani specification:**

1. **No single national standard** — NHA M-4 (motorways), LDA 2021 (Punjab arterials), CDA 2017 (Islamabad), KMC (Karachi) each use different specs. Know your authority before procurement.

2. **Generator economics** — At PKR 168/kWh diesel fuel cost, LED's 60% wattage reduction cuts DG operating cost by 60% during load-shedding. The fastest payback argument for HESCO/QESCO zones.

3. **Ta=55°C driver mandatory for Punjab/Sindh** — Generic Chinese street lights with Ta≤45°C drivers fail in 1–3 years at Multan/Jacobabad/Dadu 48–52°C ambient. Specify Meanwell ELG-240 or XLG-320 (Ta=55°C, 7-year warranty).

4. **IP66+IK08, not IP65** — IP65 gaskets in pole-top service fail within 3–5 monsoon seasons in Pakistan's humid climate. IK08 impact resistance is mandatory per NHA M-4 and increasingly by LDA/CDA.

5. **Grid voltage variation kills photocells** — WAPDA LT feeders swing 165V–270V during load-shedding cycles. Standard 180V–264V photocells false-cycle at the low end. Specify 90V–277V wide-voltage-tolerance photocell switches.

6. **Solar street lights: LiFePO4 only** — Lead-acid batteries fail in 18 months at 45°C. Specify minimum 50Ah LiFePO4 for 2-night autonomy including Pakistan's November–January low-irradiance window (3.5 kWh/m²/day Punjab).

7. **PSQCA PS 4778:2022 enforcement is real** — Three consignments seized at Karachi port Q1 2026. Municipal PPRA tenders now require PSQCA as mandatory pre-qualification. Always verify certificate number at psqca.com.pk.

EN 13201 Class ME compliance note: a luminaire can meet wattage and lumen spec but still fail EN 13201 uniformity/glare if fitted with symmetric (Type V) optics instead of the required Type II/III asymmetric road optics. This is the most common specification error in Pakistan market procurements.

For NHA motorway projects: add DALI dimming (100%/50%/30% schedule = additional 25% energy saving), 10kV built-in SPD, and 10m–12m octagonal breakaway poles per NHA M-4 specification.
