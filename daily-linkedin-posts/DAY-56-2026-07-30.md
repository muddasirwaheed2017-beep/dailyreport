**Why Pakistani Control Panels Fail WAPDA Inspection — And How PKR 2,000 in Wiring Duct Prevents a PKR 60,000 Rework**

The most common single cause of Pakistani panel rejection at WAPDA SDO inspection in 2024–2026 is not missing safety equipment or wrong circuit protection.

It is wiring duct: wrong size, overloaded, covers open, power and control cables mixed.

Here is everything a Pakistani panel builder needs to know about wiring duct before the next build:

---

**The 40% Fill Ratio — Why Pakistan Uses 35%**

EN 50085-1 (European cable duct standard) specifies that the total cable cross-section inside any duct must not exceed 40% of the duct's internal area.

The standard was written for 30°C ambient temperature.

Pakistani summer ambient: 45°C (Karachi, Multan, Lahore, Faisalabad). That 15°C difference reduces every cable's current-carrying capacity by approximately 12–18%. The 5% buffer between the European 40% and the Pakistani 35% target represents the additional thermal margin needed for reliable operation at 45°C ambient.

In practice: if your panel is sized for today, target 35% fill. This leaves room for thermal expansion, future cable additions, and the inevitable modifications every Pakistani panel sees within its first 5 years.

---

**Slotted vs Solid Duct: The Decision Made in 30 Seconds**

- **Slotted duct (grey):** Standard for all 230 VAC / 24 VDC control wiring inside the panel. Cables exit through slots to reach each MCB, contactor, relay, and terminal block. The slot interval (15–25 mm) means a cable can exit at exactly the height it needs to reach its termination.

- **Solid duct (black):** For main power cables (6 mm² and above) from the incomer circuit breaker to the main busbar, and from the busbar to outgoing feeder breakers. Also used in outdoor or IP54+ panels where slot gaps would allow dust or insects.

Most correctly-built Pakistani MCC panels use both: grey slotted for the control circuit rows, black solid for the main power spine running vertically behind the busbar assembly.

---

**Mini Duct: The Component Most Pakistani PLC Panels Are Missing**

Mini wiring duct (12–25 mm wide, 12–16 mm high) is for signal cables — the 0.5 mm² and 0.75 mm² conductors that carry 4–20 mA instrumentation signals, PLC I/O connections, RS-485 communication, and interposing relay wiring.

Why it matters: routing a 4–20 mA signal cable alongside a 1.5 mm² contactor coil cable induces 0.5–2 mA of noise through capacitive coupling. On a 16 mA full-scale current loop, this represents 3–12% measurement error. A water level sensor reading 3% high causes the pump to cycle at the wrong setpoint.

Mini duct price: PKR 120–200 per 2-metre length.
Cost of removing signal noise troubleshooting from a Pakistani process control installation: PKR 15,000–80,000 in engineer time plus a 2–5 day production loss.

---

**WAPDA SDO Inspection: 5 Duct Items That Fail Panels**

From contractor rejection data across LESCO, GEPCO, PESCO, HESCO, and MEPCO (2024–2026):

1. **Fill ratio violation** — duct cover cannot close flat because cables overflow the duct walls. Rework: PKR 8,000–35,000.
2. **Cover not seated** — duct covers partially open because cable weight pushes them off the retaining clips. Rework: PKR 3,000–10,000.
3. **Power + control cables in same duct** — 400 V and 24 VDC cables in the same duct, no physical separation. Rework: PKR 15,000–60,000.
4. **Sharp cable bends at slot exits** — cables kinked at the slot edge, outer sheath visibly compressed. Minimum bend radius for 1.5 mm² cable: 21 mm. Rework: PKR 5,000–20,000.
5. **Gaps between duct sections** — duct sections not properly joined, cables escape at uncontrolled points. Use factory corner pieces and splice covers. Rework: PKR 2,000–8,000.
