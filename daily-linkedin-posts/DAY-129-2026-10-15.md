**Servo Drive Selection in Pakistan 2026 — What Engineers Get Wrong**

Pakistani industrial automation is growing fast — CNC retrofits, laser cutters, textile machinery modernisation. But servo drive selection mistakes still cost manufacturers millions in rework, downtime, and failed installations.

Three decisions that separate successful projects from expensive failures:

**1. Inertia ratio before brand selection**
Most engineers pick a servo drive brand and power rating first. The correct sequence: calculate load-to-motor inertia ratio first (J_load/J_motor). Ratio > 20:1 means mechanical resonance and oscillation — no amount of tuning fixes a fundamentally wrong inertia match. Add a gearbox; it costs less than the rework.

**2. Absolute encoders for Pakistani power conditions**
Pakistan averages 2–4 power outages per day in many industrial areas. Incremental encoder servo systems require homing after every outage. On a CNC machine with a 3-minute homing cycle and 4 outages per day, that's 12 minutes of productive time lost daily — 52 hours per year. Multi-turn absolute encoder premium: Rs 15,000–40,000 per axis. ROI: less than 2 months.

**3. Fieldbus selection for multi-axis systems**
EtherCAT is now available from Pakistani distributors (Delta ASDA-A3, Inovance SV680N). For 3+ axis coordinated motion, EtherCAT at 125 µs cycle time eliminates the interpolation errors that plague pulse/direction systems at high speeds. The controller investment (PC-based TwinCAT or CODESYS) is recovered on the first precision job.
