**3 specification errors that cost Pakistan industrial plants money every year — and how to fix them in 10 minutes.**

Pressure switches and flow switches are the most common field devices in Pakistani electrical panels, yet they're routinely mis-specified. Here's what we see on site repeatedly:

**❌ Error 1: Wrong hysteresis → compressor short-cycling**
A 0.5 bar hysteresis on an 11 kW compressor with a 500 L tank = 32 start/stop cycles per hour. Motor starter contacts weld in weeks. The fix: wider hysteresis, or calculate the minimum receiver tank volume for your compressor output. The formula is in our new guide.

**❌ Error 2: NO contact wiring on a protection circuit**
SPDT switch, wired through the NO contact. Wire break = "no alarm" = compressor runs unprotected. The safety rule: every protection circuit must use the NC contact. Wire break = de-energised input = protective trip. Simple, but skipped on half the panels we see.

**❌ Error 3: No DP switch on the compressed air filter**
Pakistan factories replace filters on a fixed calendar schedule — monthly or quarterly, regardless of actual condition. A differential pressure switch across the coalescing filter tells you exactly when it needs replacing. Clean ΔP: 0.15–0.3 bar. Replace at 0.5 bar ΔP. You'll save money on filters you don't replace early and catch clogged filters before they strain the compressor.
