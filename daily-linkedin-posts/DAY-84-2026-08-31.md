## Topic: Cable Jointing Field Guide Pakistan 2026

---

**38% of underground cable circuit failures in Pakistan trace back to jointing workmanship defects — not the cable itself.**

That figure comes from NEPRA's 2023 distribution network reliability report, and it's consistent with what LESCO, K-Electric, FESCO and IESCO field teams deal with every year: joints made by uncertified jointers, without the right kit, in the wrong conditions, without a VLF test before energisation.

The costs compound. A joint failure at 11kV means excavation, minimum 2–3 days outage, and a repeat jointing job — plus the reputational hit if you're the contractor whose dossier was incomplete.

→ 4 mandatory tests before cutting (megger, continuity, moisture, cable identity)

**Step-by-step procedures:**
