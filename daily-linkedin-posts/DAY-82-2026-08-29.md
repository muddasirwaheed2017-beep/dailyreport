**8 things most LT cable specifications in Pakistan get wrong**

After reviewing dozens of electrical project specs across Punjab and Sindh, here are the most common errors I see engineers make when specifying underground power cables in Pakistan:

**1. Using IEC ampacity without Pakistani climate derating**
IEC 60287 reference conditions: 20°C ground temperature. Reality in Lahore peak July: 38°C ground at 750mm. That's a 10% ampacity reduction before you touch soil resistivity. In Multan peak: combined factor as low as 0.76 — a 24% cut.

**2. Ignoring soil thermal resistivity**
Dry sandy soil in Multan/Balochistan has 2.0–2.5 K·m/W resistivity — double the IEC reference. This alone costs you 13–18% ampacity. If you're specifying in rocky Quetta, you need to account for it.

**3. Not verifying DISCO burial specifications**
Minimum depth is NOT a uniform 600mm. WAPDA/DISCO standards specify: 600mm in gardens → 750mm footpaths → 1,000mm under vehicular roads → 1,200mm under highways. Using 600mm under a main road = DISCO rejection on inspection.

**4. Specifying copper when aluminium is correct**
For any conductor ≥16mm², aluminium is 60–65% cheaper and fully DISCO-accepted. The only rules: bi-metallic lugs at copper terminal interfaces + anti-oxidant compound at all joints. That's a PKR 3,000–10,000/metre saving on large cross-sections.

**5. Not checking 11kV cable screen short-circuit rating**
LESCO's 11kV fault level is 500–630MVA (26–33kA). A 3C×95mm² aluminium 11kV cable has a 7.6kA 1-second screen rating — marginal at LESCO. Go to 150mm² minimum for LESCO points. I see consultants missing this — and the cable screen burns on the first fault.

**6. Using non-WAPDA-AVL manufacturers**
DISCOs maintain Approved Vendor Lists. Cables from non-listed manufacturers are rejected at inspection regardless of price or paper certification. Always confirm AVL status before specifying any imported cable.

**7. No grouping derating for parallel cable runs**
Three armoured cables touching in a trench → 0.82 grouping factor. Four → 0.75. Many specs I review specify 240mm² for a 350A load, with three circuits in the same trench — and forget that 385A × 0.82 = 316A. Just enough. Until it's a hot summer.

**8. No import duty analysis on imported cables**
Chinese/Turkish/Malaysian cable looks cheaper ex-works. But add CD 5% + ACD 2% + GST 18% + WHT 4.5% + port handling + inland freight: landed price is typically 30–45% above CIF. Domestic Newage/PakCable is usually cheaper on a landed basis for standard sizes.
