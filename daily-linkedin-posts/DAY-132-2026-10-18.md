**How load-shedding is silently destroying your couplings — and the Pakistan-specific fix**

Most coupling failures in Pakistani industrial plants are not caused by overloading or misalignment.

They're caused by a factor that doesn't appear in any global selection chart: **LESCO/FESCO/QESCO load-shedding restart cycles**.

Here's the engineering reality:

When a motor stops mid-load during a power cut and restarts, it draws 600–800% of full-load current and produces 4–6× nominal torque for 3–5 electrical cycles. A jaw coupling rated for 1.5× nominal can handle that once.

But a Faisalabad textile mill with 5 load-shedding events per day means **1,825 high-shock restart events per year**. A coupling with no margin fails within months.

**The Pakistan Service Factor method:**

For any application with ≥4 restarts/day, add +0.25 to your standard service factor before selecting coupling size. For 48°C+ ambient locations (Multan, DG Khan, Rahimyar Khan summer), add another +0.10 for soft PU spider softening.

A 30 kW pump that "needs" a Jaw L190 by standard selection → actually needs Tyre F-80 in Pakistani conditions.
