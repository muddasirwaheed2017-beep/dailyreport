**The 7 failure modes unique to Pakistan that IEC datasheets don't cover:**

1. **PSQCA PS 4042 conductor resistance failure (38% market failure rate)** — Undersized copper cross-sections are mislabelled. Physical verification required on every drum.

2. **Load-shedding thermal cycling** — Pakistan's 4,200–7,300 annual power cycles vs IEC's assumed 30 cause PVC insulation cracking at back-box entry points. Standard Type A1 PVC is not rated for this regime.

3. **45–50°C ambient derating** — Loft runs in Pakistan summer hit 55–65°C. The 0.71× derating factor means your "2.5mm² socket cable" is effectively a 16A cable in a 20A-protected circuit.

4. **WAPDA service connection minimum** — WAPDA 2022 regulations require 16mm² aluminium (minimum) for 30–60A single-phase service. Most failed WAPDA inspections in residential projects involve undersized meter tails.

5. **Karachi coastal grade** — Marine atmosphere within 5km of the Arabian Sea accelerates PVC plasticizer loss. 44% of coastal Karachi cables older than 10 years show brittle insulation failure (PSQCA 2024 survey).

6. **NBC 2021 conduit mandate** — All concealed wiring must be in PSQCA-approved conduit. Bare single-core in plastered walls is a building code violation — and a rewiring nightmare.

7. **LSZH/FR-PVC gap in high-rise and institutional buildings** — SBCA Karachi and PEPD Punjab fire codes require FR-PVC or LSZH above G+4 floors. 90%+ of Pakistani market supply is standard PVC, which emits toxic HCl on combustion and fails horizontal flame-spread tests.
