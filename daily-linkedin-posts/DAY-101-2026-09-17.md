**7 UPS failures we see repeatedly in Pakistani industrial plants — and how to prevent them**

After reviewing hundreds of industrial UPS installations across Lahore, Karachi, Faisalabad, and Peshawar, the same failure patterns appear again and again.

Here are the seven most costly ones — and the fixes that actually work in Pakistan's grid conditions:

**1. Buying line-interactive for NTDC protection panels**
NTDC PMM-P-2024 mandates VFI class (online double-conversion). A VI-class UPS has 2–4 ms transfer time — enough to reset a numerical relay watchdog during load-shedding initiation. The fix is simple: check the IEC 62040-3 classification. VFI-SS-111 = compliant. Anything else = reject it.

**2. Using 5-year VRLA in 45°C battery rooms**
Every 10°C above 25°C halves VRLA life (Arrhenius law). A 5-year cell at 45°C gives 2–3 years service. A 10-year cell gives 4–6 years. The lifecycle economics favour 10-year cells for almost every industrial application. NTDC PMM-P-2024 mandates 10-year design life for protection panel UPS.

**3. No temperature compensation on float voltage**
A fixed float of 2.27V/cell at 25°C undercharges at 48°C summer and sulfates at 5°C Quetta winter. Temperature-compensated float (driven by a probe IN the battery enclosure, not the UPS ambient sensor) is non-negotiable for Pakistan's climate range.

**4. Trusting the "Battery OK" indicator**
UPS indicators measure voltage — not capacity. A fully sulfated VRLA string shows the correct float voltage but delivers 15 minutes instead of 60. The only reliable check: periodic discharge testing to 80% DoD or BMS impedance spectroscopy. Implement one of these.

**5. Wide input voltage range: the spec nobody verifies**
Pakistani 11 kV feeders sag to 170–190V during load-shedding initiation. UPS units with 160–265V input range go into bypass exactly when you need them most. Specify 120–280V input range (verified on actual test data, not the headline spec).

**6. Parallel battery strings without individual fusing**
Adding a second VRLA string without individual fuses creates a catastrophic fault path. A shorted string pulls full discharge current from the healthy string. Each parallel string must have its own string fuse.

**7. Skipping the generator compatibility test**
Small diesel generators produce 8–15% THD and frequency hunting. Some UPS rectifiers become unstable on generator input. Always test under full load before commissioning. If instability occurs, add a generator-to-UPS isolation transformer.
