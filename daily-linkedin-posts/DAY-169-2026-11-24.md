## Post

**Pakistan's industrial motor base has a PKR multi-billion problem — and most engineers are still ignoring it.**

Over 90% of Pakistan's large industrial motors (above 15kW) are still started by Direct-On-Line (DOL) or star-delta starters.

Every DOL motor start draws 600–800% of full-load current for 3–8 seconds.

For a 55kW pump at 415V, that's a 540 kVA demand spike every time the pump starts.

At WAPDA's 2026 C1 tariff of PKR 2,750/kVA/month, if that startup sets your monthly peak demand — you're paying an extra PKR 1.5M+ per year.

**An electronic soft starter changes the picture entirely:**

✅ Reduces starting inrush to 200–350% FLC (from 600–800%)
✅ Eliminates water hammer — 70–90% pressure spike reduction on pump trips
✅ Extends motor and pump bearing life — no mechanical shock on every start
✅ Soft-stop function decelerates centrifugal pumps gradually — protecting pipes, valves, and seals
