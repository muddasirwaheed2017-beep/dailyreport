**Pakistani industrial facilities paid an estimated PKR 4.2 billion in avoidable WAPDA demand charges in 2025. Here's the technology that eliminates this cost.**

WAPDA's billing structure creates an asymmetric penalty: your monthly demand charge is set by the **single highest 15-minute interval** in the billing month. If your factory runs at 850 kW all month but spikes to 1,050 kW for just one 15-minute interval — during morning shift startup, chiller restart after a trip, or simultaneous equipment commissioning — your entire month's demand charge is calculated at 1,050 kW.

At LESCO B-3 tariff, every 100 kVA of overshoot costs PKR 58,000–78,000 per month. Permanently. Month after month.

**An Automatic Demand Response (ADR) controller eliminates this permanently.**

The logic is straightforward: the controller monitors your real-time kW every second, projects whether you will overshoot your demand target by the end of the current 15-minute WAPDA interval, and automatically sheds non-critical loads before the interval closes. When headroom reopens, it restores loads in reverse priority order.

At a 1,000 kVA LESCO B-3 textile mill with 267 kW of sheddable capacity (non-critical lighting, office AC, compressor top-up, chiller partial unload, secondary conveyor, water treatment pump), a mid-range PLC-based ADR system costs PKR 420,000 installed and delivers:

- PKR 78,000/month in demand charge avoidance
- PKR 42,000/month in ToU energy shift saving (peak → off-peak load deferral)
- **Payback: 3.5 months. Then pure saving forever.**

**The NEPRA DSM dimension:** Pakistan's 2024 Demand Side Management Regulations opened a second revenue stream. Factories with ≥1 MW contracted demand that register as Demand Response Providers with NTDC earn capacity payments — estimated PKR 350–550/kW/month — for maintaining dispatchable curtailment capability. A 1 MW factory with an IEC 61850-interfaced ADR controller can earn PKR 350,000–550,000/month in NTDC capacity payments on top of the WAPDA bill saving.

**The generator protection dimension:** Most Pakistani factories operate gensets sized at 50–70% of grid contracted demand. When the ATS transfers during a WAPDA outage, the full plant load can overload the genset in seconds. An ADR controller integrated with the ATS pre-sheds non-critical loads within 5 seconds of detecting a supply interruption — the genset receives a manageable load at transfer.

One controller. Three revenue/protection streams. No production sacrifice when properly engineered with process interlocks.

If your facility is on LESCO/FESCO/GEPCO B-2 or B-3 tariff and pays demand charges every month without an ADR system, this is the highest-ROI electrical investment available to you in 2026.
