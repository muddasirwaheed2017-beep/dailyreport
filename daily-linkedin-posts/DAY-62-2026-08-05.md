# DAY-62 LinkedIn Post — 2026-08-05

## Post (Long-form, engineering audience)

**847 electrocution deaths in Pakistan's consumer network in FY2024–25. The NEPRA State of Industry Report links 71% to inadequate building earthing.**

Yet most Pakistan electrical contractors still treat earthing as "hammer a rod into wet soil and call it done."

Here's what's actually happening — and why it matters more than ever in 2026:

---

**The TT system misunderstanding killing people**

Every WAPDA/DISCO customer is on a TT earthing system (IEC 60364-1). The utility transformer neutral is earthed at the pole. Your building metalwork must connect to a *separate, independent earth electrode* that belongs to your installation only.

What many local contractors do instead: connect the neutral bar and earth bar together at the DB board.

On a TT supply, this does not create TN-C-S. It creates a situation where if the WAPDA neutral breaks during a load-shedding restoration surge (common — Pakistan's neutral return path is heavily loaded), all earthed metalwork in your building rises to 220 V above ground potential. Every metal socket pin. Every panel door. Every equipment chassis.

Pakistan records dozens of fatalities from this exact scenario every year.

---

**The seasonal soil trap**

Central Punjab (Lahore, Faisalabad, Multan) alluvial soil resistivity:
- August monsoon: 20–60 Ω·m → your 1.5 m copper-clad rod reads 4 Ω ✅
- May pre-monsoon: 100–300 Ω·m → same rod reads 25–35 Ω ❌

WAPDA Distribution Code requires ≤10 Ω. Test in April–June, not August. Use the 4-probe Wenner method for system-level assessment, not a 3-terminal spot check.

---

**Solar earthing: NEPRA's 2025 finding**

NEPRA SQ&P inspectors found earth resistance >20 Ω in **34% of net metering installations** inspected in FY2024–25.

Your solar system's RCCB Type A will not provide earth fault protection if the earth resistance is 20 Ω. Your SPD cannot discharge a lightning transient safely to a 20 Ω earth. Your transformerless inverter's DC GFI function depends on a solid earth reference to detect insulation failure.

NEPRA now requires measured (not calculated) ≤10 Ω, submitted with the net metering application.

---

**The IT system cases**

Pakistan's private hospital sector (AKU, SKMH, CMH, and 400+ private hospitals) is required under IEC 60364-7-710 to use IT earthing (isolated neutral) for operating theatre and ICU circuits. An IT system continues powering equipment through the first insulation fault — the surgeon's equipment doesn't go dark when a cable develops a ground fault. An Insulation Monitoring Device (IMD) alarms; the engineer finds and fixes the fault; the circuit never disconnected.

For data centres (PTCL, NRTC, private carrier-neutral): IT on the critical busways for the same reason — zero unscheduled interruption on first fault.
