**Rationale:** D40 covers conduit pipe (PVC rigid, flexible, GI, medium-duty). These are the most under-specified items in Pakistani electrical installations and the root cause of the majority of conduit system failures (bushing missing, wrong IP rating, no expansion coupling, corrosion from wrong material spec).

4. **LESCO 2025 SQ&P bushing defect finding** — missing bushing at meter board entry is the most common inspection defect; bushing cost PKR 20–50 vs rewiring cost PKR 50,000–250,000 (first documented link)
