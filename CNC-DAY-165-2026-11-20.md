**Pakistan 2026 Differentiators:**
1. NTRC EV Policy 2023 mandates Type 2 / CCS2 — GB/T Chinese imports incompatible with public EVSE
2. LESCO EV-1 off-peak PKR 24.5/kWh — EV per-km cost PKR 2.5–4 vs petrol PKR 8–11
3. LESCO voltage sag at peak hours (360V) causes DC charger derating 15–25% — specify 320–480V input range or fit AVR
4. Type A RCCB does NOT detect DC smooth leakage from EV OBCs — Type B mandatory (PSQCA PS:5201:2023)
5. OCPP 1.6J lacks smart charging profiles needed for NTRC 2027 mandate — specify 2.0.1 now
6. CPO demand charge trap: 60kW charger = PKR 51,000/month fixed capacity even at 2h/day use — OCPP DLM essential
7. 4G OCPP connectivity: use OCPP-J (not SOAP) — SOAP breaks on cellular NAT
8. PSQCA PS:5201:2023 conformity mark required — uncertified EVSE may be held at customs
