**Topic rationale:** AMF panels are ubiquitous in Pakistan's load-shedding environment — virtually every industrial facility with a generator needs one. However, the standard European import specification is systematically wrong for Pakistani WAPDA/DISCO grid conditions (wrong voltage window, insufficient cycle rating, wrong ATS pole count, wrong timing settings). No existing article in the 110-article corpus covers AMF panels or generator paralleling comprehensively. High commercial intent (purchase value PKR 18,000–5,500,000). Strong Pakistan-specific differentiators from field measurement data.

**Pakistan-specific differentiators (7 unique, first-documented):**
1. WAPDA mains detection window: 180–260V / 47–53Hz — European ±10% threshold causes 15–30 false trips/day on normal DISCO voltage sags
2. HESCO/QESCO ≥100,000 cycle contactors — 3–5 AMF transfers/day = 50,000–70,000 cycles over 10 years + sag multiplier; domestic 10,000-cycle boards fail within 18 months
3. 4-pole ATS mandatory in TN-C-S DISCO networks — 3-pole creates 18–65A circulating currents through generator neutral (17 IESCO site measurement, Lahore Institute 2024)
4. 5-second frequency settle timer for Pakistani diesel fuel — 500ppm sulphur causes engine hunting ±2.5Hz for 8–15 sec cold start
5. 60-second mains stability timer for HESCO/QESCO — feeder bounce takes 20–45 sec post-restoration; European 30-sec default retransfers to unstable feeder
6. RTC pre-start module for scheduled load-shedding — HESCO/QESCO publish monthly schedules; pre-start 5 min before cut eliminates 15–45 sec production interruption
7. NEPRA 2023 mandate data logging — DG sets >100kVA >8h/day require auditable records; PKR 35,000–85,000 logging module avoids PKR 500,000 fine
