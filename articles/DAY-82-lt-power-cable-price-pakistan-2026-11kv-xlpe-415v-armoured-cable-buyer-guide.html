2. 415V LT Armoured Cable — Specifications & Ampacity

Standard 415V LT armoured cable in Pakistan conforms to IEC 60502-1 (up to 1kV) with steel wire armour (SWA) or steel tape armour (STA). XLPE insulation is preferred over PVC for high-load feeders (higher conductor temperature limit: 90°C vs 70°C). Conductor is aluminium for ≥16mm² and copper for smaller cross-sections or where contractually specified.

3. 11kV XLPE Cable — Specifications & Ampacity

11kV XLPE cable in Pakistan conforms to IEC 60502-2 (1–30kV). Standard construction: stranded aluminium (or copper) conductor, semi-conductive conductor screen, XLPE insulation, semi-conductive insulation screen, copper tape screen, PVC inner sheath, galvanised steel wire armour (SWA), PVC outer sheath. Voltage rating: 6.35/11kV (Uo/U).

How to use: Multiply the IEC standard ampacity by the combined derating factor for your site conditions. Example: 4C × 240mm² aluminium XLPE direct-buried, IEC ampacity 385A. 13. Installation: Jointing, Termination & Gland Selection

Cable Termination — 415V LT Armoured

  • Use heat-shrink or cold-shrink indoor/outdoor termination kits rated 1kV (IEC 60502-4)
  • Indoor (panel room): single-layer heat-shrink finger kit + aluminium compression lug (bi-metallic if connecting to copper busbar)
  • Outdoor (kiosk, outdoor MDB): use cold-shrink outdoor termination kit + IP66 IP68 cable gland with armour clamp
  • SWA armour must be bonded to earth at both ends for LT feeders — use earth bonding clamp at gland, connect to earthing pit

Cable Termination — 11kV XLPE

  • Indoor terminations (VCB/RMU panels): use manufacturer-matched cold-shrink or heat-shrink stress cone kits rated 12kV (IEC 60502-4 / IEC 60840)
  • Outdoor terminations: use outdoor termination kits with weathershed (porcelain or silicone) rated 12kV / 100kV BIL
  • Copper screen bond: connect to HV earth system via tinned copper earth braids — never leave floating
  • In Karachi coastal: use silicone-rubber weathershed outdoor terminators (better tracking resistance than porcelain in salt-laden humid air)

Cable Joints

  • 415V straight joint: heat-shrink or cold-shrink inline joint kit (IEC 60502-4). Cold-shrink preferred in humid environments.
  • 11kV straight joint: cold-shrink 3-core heat-shrink joint kit (12kV rated). Each joint must be in a brick-lined joint pit — not in backfill.
  • Minimise joints — pull continuous drums where possible. Drum lengths: 4C × 240mm² typically 200–500m per drum depending on weight.

14. FAQ — 8 Most-Asked Questions About LT Power Cable in Pakistan

Q1: What is the price of 4-core 240mm² armoured cable in Pakistan 2026?

4-core 240mm² XLPE/PVC SWA armoured cable (aluminium conductor) costs PKR 6,800–8,500 per metre in Pakistan in August 2026, depending on conductor material and jacket type. Copper conductor equivalent is PKR 18,500–22,000/metre. Prices vary with USD/PKR rate and LME copper/aluminium.

Q2: What is the minimum burial depth for LT cable in Pakistan?

WAPDA/DISCO minimum burial depth for 415V LT underground feeder: 600mm in footpaths and gardens, 750mm under lightly trafficked roads, 1,000mm under vehicular roads, 1,200mm under major roads and highways. 100mm sand bedding below and above is mandatory, plus a concrete protective tile and red warning tape 250mm above tile.

Q3: Which cable manufacturers are approved by WAPDA in Pakistan?

Historically recognised DISCO-AVL manufacturers include Newage Cables (Lahore), PakCable/Pakistan Cables (Karachi), and SuperCable. Always obtain the current DISCO-specific AVL from your local DISCO office before specification — the list is updated periodically.

Q4: How much does 11kV XLPE cable cost in Pakistan?

11kV XLPE armoured cable in Pakistan 2026: 3-core 95mm² PKR 2,200–3,800/metre; 3-core 240mm² PKR 5,500–8,000/metre; 3-core 400mm² PKR 9,000–14,000/metre. Copper conductor: 3–4× aluminium price. Prices include GST 18% but exclude installation and termination kits.

Q5: What is the derating factor for underground cable in Pakistani summer?

Pakistani ground temperature at 750mm depth reaches 32–40°C in peak summer (Punjab/Sindh vs IEC 20°C reference). Combined temperature + soil resistivity derating ranges from 0.76 (Multan dry sandy peak) to 1.03 (Karachi coastal clay). Apply site-specific factors before final cable cross-section selection.

Q6: Can I use aluminium conductor cable for LT power distribution in Pakistan?

Yes — aluminium is standard for LT feeder and sub-main distribution above 16mm². Aluminium costs 60–65% less than copper equivalent. Use bi-metallic lugs at copper terminal interfaces, apply anti-oxidant compound at all joints, and use moisture-barrier sheath in Karachi coastal zones.

Q7: What is the ampacity of 4-core 185mm² aluminium XLPE cable in Pakistani conditions?

IEC standard ampacity: 335A (20°C ground). After applying Lahore peak summer combined derating 0.84: approximately 281A. With 3-circuit grouping (0.82): approximately 230A. Always calculate site-specific derating per IEC 60287.

Q8: What cable jointing kit should I use for 11kV XLPE cable in Pakistan?

Use cold-shrink or heat-shrink 3-core straight joints rated 12kV (IEC 60502-4 / IEC 60840). Cold-shrink preferred in Karachi humidity. Transition joints (XLPE to PILC) require stress cones — confirm kit compatibility with cable insulation type before purchase.
