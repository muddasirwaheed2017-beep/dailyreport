4. Stress Cones — What They Do and Why They Must Be Right

The stress cone is arguably the most critical component in any 11kV termination. Understanding it is essential before specifying or installing any MV cable accessory.

When the cable insulation is cut back to expose the conductor lug, the boundary between the semiconducting screen and the insulation creates a sharp discontinuity in the electric field. At 11kV, this discontinuity produces field intensities that can exceed 10–15 kV/mm — far above air's dielectric strength (~3 kV/mm) — and initiate partial discharge (PD). PD erodes the insulation surface, produces ozone and nitrogen oxides, and eventually causes tracking to earth or flashover.

🔴 Critical Rule: A stress cone is mandatory in every 11kV termination. No exceptions. Terminating 11kV cable by simply cutting back insulation and clamping a lug — without a stress cone — will cause failure. The only question is when: within months in outdoor or humid conditions, within 1–3 years even in dry indoor environments.

8. Link Boxes & Screen Bonding — Single-Point vs Cross-Bonding PK-FIRST

For 11kV cables longer than ~500m (3-core) or where the cable carries significant load current through a long single-core route, the metallic screen (copper tape or copper wire) of the cable carries an induced voltage due to electromagnetic coupling with the conductor. Unmanaged, this sheath voltage can reach hundreds of volts at the open end — dangerous to touch and a source of continuous circulating current losses.

14. FAQ — 8 Most-Asked Questions in Pakistan

Q1. What is the price of an 11kV cable joint kit in Pakistan 2026?

11kV cable joint kit price in Pakistan 2026: straight cold shrink joint (3-core 95–240mm²) PKR 12,000–22,000 per kit; straight heat shrink joint PKR 9,500–18,000; trifurcating cold shrink joint PKR 28,000–45,000. XLPE-to-PILC transition joints add 30–50% to base price. All prices include GST 18%.

Q2. Cold shrink vs heat shrink — which 11kV joint kit is better for Pakistan?

Cold shrink preferred in Karachi (humidity, no open flame in manholes). Heat shrink standard in Punjab inland (lower cost, field-proven, requires propane torch). Pre-moulded is highest quality for direct-buried DISCO-feeder service. Both cold and heat shrink meet IEC 60502-4 when correctly installed from a certified kit.

Q3. What is a stress cone and why is it needed?

The stress cone manages the electric field at the semicon screen cut-back point in an MV termination. Without it, field intensity exceeds air's dielectric strength at 11kV, causing partial discharge, tracking, and eventual flashover. It is mandatory in every 11kV termination — no exceptions.

Q4. Do I need a link box for my 11kV cable?

For routes <500m (3-core XLPE), single-point bonding without a link box is standard. For longer routes or cross-bonded systems (city ring mains), link boxes with sheath voltage limiters (SVL) are required. Always confirm with your DISCO before ordering.

Q5. How long does an 11kV cable joint take to install?

Cold or heat shrink straight joint on 3-core 240mm²: 4–6 hours field installation. Pre-moulded joint: 3–4 hours. XLPE-to-PILC transition joint: 6–8 hours. Add rest-break time in summer above 45°C ambient (Lahore, Multan, Karachi June–August).

Q6. What are DISCO's inspection requirements for 11kV cable joints in Pakistan?

DISCO (LESCO, K-Electric, FESCO, IESCO) requirements: kit must be on DISCO AVL; jointer must be DISCO-registered; inspector witnesses first joint; post-installation VLF or tan-delta test at 1.73×Uo (19kV) for new HT connections; as-built GPS-tagged cable route drawings submitted within 30 days of commissioning.

Q7. Can I use indoor MV terminations outdoors in Karachi?

No. Indoor terminations are not rated for outdoor exposure (rain, salt, UV). Karachi coastal outdoor terminations require outdoor-rated kits with silicone shed design and minimum 31mm/kV creepage (Class C pollution). Using indoor kits outdoors in Karachi will result in tracking failure within 6–18 months.

Q8. What causes 11kV cable joint failure in Pakistan?

Top causes: (1) Wrong semicon cut-back length — 32% of failures; (2) Moisture ingress in flooded trenches (Lahore monsoon, Karachi coastal) — 24%; (3) Uncertified kit — 18%; (4) Under-crimped ferrule — 12%; (5) Wrong kit for cable type — 8%; (6) Heat damage in summer — 6%. All preventable with certified kits, trained jointers, and correct procedure.
