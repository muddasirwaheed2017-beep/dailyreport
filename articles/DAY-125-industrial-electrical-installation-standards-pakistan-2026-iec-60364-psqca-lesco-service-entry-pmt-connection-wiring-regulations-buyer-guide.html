Industrial Electrical Installation Standards Pakistan 2026 — IEC 60364, PSQCA PS 3646, LESCO Service Entry, PMT Connection & Wiring Regulations Buyer Guide

Pakistan's industrial and commercial electrical installations are governed by a layered framework: PSQCA PS 3646 (national wiring code), IEC 60364 (international standard adopted by reference), NEPRA Grid Code 2023, and individual DISCO connection regulations (LESCO, K-Electric, FESCO, IESCO, MEPCO, PESCO, HESCO, SEPCO, QESCO, TESCO, GEPCO). Getting compliant means knowing which standard governs which layer — from the PMT pole-mounted transformer down to the final socket outlet.

This guide covers the complete regulatory framework, LESCO service entry requirements, PMT connection specifications, approved cable schedules, earthing rules, and a PKR 2026 price table for compliant installation materials.

Important: PSQCA PS 3646 is a voluntary Pakistan Standard based on IEC 60364, but provincial inspectorates and DISCOs cross-reference it for compliance checks. In practice it is effectively mandatory for commercial and industrial installations.

2. IEC 60364 in Pakistan — Which Parts Apply

IEC 60364 is a multi-part standard for low-voltage electrical installations. Pakistan adopts it by reference through PSQCA PS 3646. Key parts with local relevance:

IEC 60364 PartSubjectPakistan Relevance
60364-1Fundamental principles, assessment of general characteristics, definitionsScope, voltage bands (LV = ≤1,000 V AC), system types (TN-S, TN-C, TT)
60364-4-41Protection against electric shock — ADS (Automatic Disconnection of Supply)Defines 0.4 s / 5 s disconnection times; applied by PSQCA PS 3646 Ch. 4
60364-4-43Protection against overcurrentCable-circuit co-ordination; MCCB/MCB/fuse selection per load current
60364-5-52Selection & erection of wiring systemsCable cross-section tables (current-carrying capacity at 40°C Pakistani ambient)
60364-5-54Earthing arrangements & protective conductorsPE sizing (Cu ≥ 16 mm² for ≥ 25 mm² phase), bonding requirements
60364-6Verification (testing)Insulation resistance ≥ 1 MΩ @ 500 V dc; continuity; RCD trip time
60364-7-714External lighting installationsOutdoor industrial lighting, road lighting, high-mast systems

Correction factor for Pakistan: IEC 60364-5-52 tables assume 30°C ambient. Pakistan summer ambient commonly reaches 45–50°C; apply correction factor Ct ≈ 0.75–0.82 to rated cable current. This results in one or two cross-section steps up from the table value for industrial installations.

3. PSQCA PS 3646 — Pakistan's National Wiring Code

3.1 Structure

PS 3646 mirrors IEC 60364 structure with Pakistan-specific annexures covering:

  • Approved conductor materials: copper (primary), aluminium (restricted to specific applications)
  • Minimum cross-sections: 1.5 mm² Cu for final circuits; 4 mm² Cu for ring mains; 16 mm² for submain feeders
  • Conduit fill ratios: 40% maximum for PVC conduit; 35% for GI conduit
  • Cable spacing from water pipes: ≥ 150 mm; from gas pipes: ≥ 300 mm
  • Wiring height in wet/industrial areas: no horizontal cable runs below 150 mm from floor

3.3 Socket Outlet & Plug Standards

  • Pakistan residential: BS 1363 (3-pin rectangular, 13 A) — inherited from British era
  • Industrial: IEC 60309 (CEE form) 16 A / 32 A / 63 A, 415 V 3P+N+E (IP44 minimum)
  • Mandatory: shuttered sockets in residential installations (PSQCA PS 3646 §553.1.2)
  • Prohibited: 2-pin unprotected sockets in new installations since 2019 amendment

4.2 Service Entry Cable Specifications (LV)

  • 1-phase service drop (overhead): LESCO-approved 2-core 16 mm² Cu XLPE service cable or 2×16 mm² Al ABC (Aerial Bundled Cable)
  • 3-phase overhead: 4-core 25 mm² Cu XLPE or 4-core Al ABC (4×25 mm² or 4×35 mm²)
  • Underground service: 4-core 25 mm² Cu XLPE armoured (SWA) or 4-core 35 mm² Al SWA; laid in LESCO-approved trench (600 mm depth, 150 mm sand bedding, warning tape)
  • Service head: LESCO-type isolating switch (100 A or 200 A, as sanctioned) in sealed LESCO-supplied enclosure
  • Meter placement: Within 3 m of service head; outdoor or accessible to LESCO meter reader; tamper-proof enclosure

4.3 Required Documentation (LESCO Connection Gate)

  1. Completed application form (online via LESCO Customer Portal or at LESCO SDO office)
  2. Ownership documents (copy of property title / lease deed)
  3. CNIC copy of applicant
  4. Wiring completion certificate from licensed Electrical Inspector (EI) — mandatory for commercial/industrial
  5. Single-line diagram (SLD) — mandatory for ≥ 10 kW commercial / all industrial
  6. NOC from local municipal authority (for new construction)
  7. Load schedule signed by licensed electrical contractor (PEC-registered for industrial)
  8. Payment of connection charges + security deposit (load-dependent)

5. PMT (Pole-Mounted Transformer) Connection Specifications

5.2 LV Side Connection (PMT to Consumer MDB)

  • LV bushing: 4-stud (3-phase + neutral) LV bushing; Al/Cu terminal lugs
  • LV cable from PMT: 4-core Al XLPE armoured, cross-section = transformer kVA ÷ 0.7 (kVA → A at 415 V 0.8 p.f.): 100 kVA → 139 A → 95 mm² Al; 200 kVA → 278 A → 150 mm² Al; 315 kVA → 437 A → 240 mm² Al
  • LV cut-out: LESCO-sealed 3-phase cut-out fuse (BS 88 HRC) at consumer service head; sized 1.25× FLC of transformer secondary
  • Neutral earthing: PMT neutral solidly bonded to earth at pole base (TN-C); consumer-side MET (Main Earthing Terminal) bonds N to local earth electrode for TN-C-S system
  • Street cabinet (if used): GRP or steel IP54 enclosure; LESCO padlock; 4-way LV distribution fuse board

5.3 Earth Electrode at PMT Pole

  • Minimum: 2 × 16 mm Cu earth rods, 1.5 m length, 2 m separation, measured resistance ≤ 1 Ω (DISCO spec)
  • Bonded to: transformer tank, LV neutral, cable armour, pole reinforcement
  • Measured with Megger Earth Clamp or 3-point fall-of-potential method
  • Annual re-measurement required per WAPDA O&M manual

8. Earthing & Bonding Requirements Under Pakistan Code

8.1 Main Earthing Terminal (MET) Requirements

  • MET connects: PE conductor from service head, main bonding conductors (gas, water, structural steel), earthing conductor to earth electrode
  • MET must be accessible, clearly labelled, and not removable without tools
  • For TN-C-S: PEN split at MET; N bar separated from PE bar downstream

8.3 Earth Electrode Requirements

  • Minimum 2 earth rods (16 mm Cu-bonded or 16 mm GI): 1.5 m minimum depth; 2 m minimum separation
  • Target resistance: ≤ 10 Ω for TT systems; ≤ 1 Ω for TN-C-S service headend; ≤ 0.5 Ω for HV substation
  • Soil resistivity in Lahore plain: typically 20–50 Ω·m; Karachi coastal: 5–15 Ω·m; Balochistan rocky: 200–500 Ω·m
  • For high-resistivity soil: use chemical earth electrodes (copper sulphate fill), bentonite backfill, or extended earth mat

11. Obtaining an Electrical Inspector (EI) Completion Certificate

Before LESCO/DISCO energises a commercial or industrial installation, an Electrical Inspector (EI) must issue a completion/fitness certificate. Process:

  1. Hire a PEC-licensed Electrical Contractor (category C-8 or higher for industrial) — PEC Register at pec.org.pk
  2. Complete wiring and testing per PS 3646: insulation resistance ≥ 1 MΩ; earth continuity ≤ 0.5 Ω; RCD test ≤ 40 ms at rated tripping current
  3. Submit EI inspection request to the provincial Chief Inspector of Electricity office (Punjab: PIDE, Sindh: Energy Department)
  4. Inspector visits and witnesses testing; signs Form EI-1 (or provincial equivalent)
  5. EI Certificate (Form EI-2) issued; submit copy to LESCO with connection application
  6. LESCO energises within 15 working days of complete documentation (NEPRA Distribution Code SLA)
