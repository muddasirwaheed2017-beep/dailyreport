12. EVSE Installation Checklist for Pakistan

  1. LESCO/DISCO load assessment: Submit load increase application for any EVSE >5kVA. For 3-phase 22kW+ or DC charger, request transformer capacity study from LESCO before civil works.
  2. NTRC registration: Private EVSE (home, office, fleet) = NTRC Private Registration (online, free). Public CPO = NTRC CPO Site Licence (Class A/B/C) + site marking standards compliance.
  3. PSQCA PS:5201:2023 compliance: Import or locally assembled EVSE must carry PSQCA conformity mark. Check PSQCA approved product list before purchase — uncertified imports may be held at customs.
  4. RCCB Type B: Install Type B RCD (not Type A) for all AC Mode 3 chargers — Type A does not protect against DC smooth ground-fault current produced by some EV onboard chargers (OBCs).
  5. Dedicated circuit: Never share EVSE circuit with other loads — EV chargers draw near-rated current continuously for 1–8 hours; shared circuits cause nuisance tripping and cable heating.
  6. OCPP backend commissioning test: Before go-live, test: (a) charger registers with CPMS, (b) start/stop transaction via app, (c) smart charging profile receives setpoint, (d) meter values transmit correctly, (e) 4G failover triggers and reconnects.
  7. Earthing verification: Measure earth resistance with earth tester (Rearth <10Ω). Re-test after monsoon and in dry season — soil resistivity variation of 3–5× is common in Pakistan plains soil.

14. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of a Level 2 EV charging station in Pakistan 2026?

A 7kW single-phase Level 2 EVSE (Type 2 socket, OCPP 2.0) costs PKR 85,000–145,000 ex-duties in Pakistan in 2026. A 22kW three-phase wallbox costs PKR 180,000–320,000. Prices include the charger hardware only — civil, cabling, and LESCO/WAPDA connection add PKR 50,000–200,000 depending on distance and transformer upgrade needs.

Which EV connector standard is used in Pakistan?

Pakistan's NTRC EV Policy 2020 (amended 2023) mandates Type 2 (IEC 62196-2) for AC charging and CCS2 (Combined Charging System) for DC fast charging. CHAdeMO legacy connectors are permitted but no new chargers are required to include them. Chinese EVs sold in Pakistan may use GB/T 20234 — confirm connector type before purchasing charging infrastructure.

What is LESCO's EV tariff rate in Pakistan 2026?

NEPRA approved the EV-1 tariff category in 2024. LESCO applies: Off-peak (11pm–7am) PKR 24.5/kWh; Peak (5pm–11pm) PKR 42/kWh; Off-peak (7am–5pm) PKR 29/kWh. Commercial EV charging stations (CPO) qualify for a fixed monthly capacity charge of PKR 850/kVA instead of residential rates. Contact your DISCO for the latest notified rates.

Can I charge an EV from solar + battery storage in Pakistan during load shedding?

Yes. A 10kWh LiFePO4 battery pack (48V 200Ah) can deliver a 7kW Level 2 charge for ~80 minutes — sufficient for 30–40 km range top-up. Connect the EVSE to your hybrid inverter's AC output with a dedicated 32A Type C MCB. Ensure the hybrid inverter is rated for 7kW continuous load and the battery BMS allows the sustained discharge current. See our Hybrid Inverter guide and BESS guide for sizing.

What OCPP version should I specify for a new EV charging project in Pakistan?

Specify OCPP 2.0.1 (ISO 15118 PnC-ready, smart charging profiles, TLS 1.2+). OCPP 1.6J chargers are cheaper but lack ISO 15118-2 Plug & Charge support and smart V2G capability. For 4G-only sites (no fibre), confirm the OCPP backend supports OCPP-J (JSON over WebSocket) over cellular. NTRC future EV charging regulations are expected to require OCPP 2.0.1 from 2027.
