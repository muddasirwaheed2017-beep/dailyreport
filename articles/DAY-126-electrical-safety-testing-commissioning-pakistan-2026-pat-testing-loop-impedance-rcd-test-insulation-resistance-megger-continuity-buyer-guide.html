Electrical Safety Testing & Commissioning Pakistan 2026 — PAT Testing, Loop Impedance, RCD Test, Insulation Resistance (Megger) & Continuity Buyer Guide

Electrical safety testing before energisation is not optional in Pakistan — the Electricity Inspector under the Pakistan Electricity Rules 1937 (Rule 45) and every DISCO's Electricity Connection Regulations require a signed test certificate before a permanent service connection is granted. This guide covers every mandatory commissioning test: Portable Appliance Testing (PAT), loop impedance measurement (Ze/Zs), RCD trip-time verification, high-voltage insulation resistance (Megger), and continuity testing (CPC/ring-main). It lists pass/fail limits, test sequences, common failure modes, and a PKR 2026 price table for the instruments a commissioning engineer in Pakistan needs.

2. Test Sequence — Correct Order Matters

IEC 60364-6 mandates the following sequence (dead tests first, live tests last). Never reverse — a continuity test on an energised circuit will damage a low-resistance tester and risks electrocution:

  1. Visual inspection (correct cable types, adequate cross-sections, correct protective devices)
  2. Continuity of protective conductors (CPC / PE)
  3. Ring-main continuity verification (if ring circuit present)
  4. Insulation resistance (IR) — all circuits dead, neutral and earth disconnected at DB
  5. Polarity (correct connections at sockets, switches in line conductor only)
  6. Earth electrode resistance (Ra) — separate test before DB energised
  7. ENERGISE installation for live tests
  8. Earth fault loop impedance (Ze at origin, Zs at furthest point of each circuit)
  9. RCD / RCCB / RCBO trip-time and trip-current verification
  10. Functional tests (MCBs, contactors, timers, interlocks)
  11. PAT testing of portable appliances (after permanent wiring tests)

3. Continuity Testing (CPC & Ring Main)

3a. Protective Conductor (CPC) Continuity

Test between each PE / CPC terminal at every accessory (socket-outlet, luminaire, fixed appliance) and the main earth terminal (MET) at the distribution board. Use a low-resistance ohmmeter (IEC 61557-4) with test current ≥200 mA to overcome surface oxidation.

3b. Ring Main Continuity

For ring final circuits (common in older Pakistani residential and commercial buildings): connect L–E of each socket in turn with temporary link. Measure: R1+R2 end-to-end, then r1 and r2 from each socket. Verify that readings are ≈ consistent within 5% — confirms ring is complete without crossed conductors.

4b. IR Test Procedure (LV Installation)

  1. Isolate all MCBs at the distribution board. Remove all lamps, electronic ballasts, surge protective devices (SPDs), and VFDs — these cannot withstand 500 V IR test voltage.
  2. Link L + N together at DB (test L+N together vs Earth — this catches L-E and N-E simultaneously).
  3. Apply 500 V DC for 60 seconds. Record reading at 60 s.
  4. Pass: ≥1 MΩ per circuit. Whole installation result: sum of all circuit IR values must exceed (1 MΩ / number of circuits) but LESCO inspectors typically accept 1 MΩ whole-installation minimum.
  5. For motor windings (separate test with 500 V or 1 kV): see our Motor Winding Insulation Test Pakistan 2026 guide for PI ratio methodology.

5. Earth Electrode Resistance (Ra) Testing

Pakistan's earthing requirements (PSQCA PS 3646 / IEC 60364-5-54) specify Ra ≤ 200 Ω for TT systems, Ra ≤ 10 Ω for TN systems (MDB earth), and Ra ≤ 1 Ω for HV substations (NTDC/DISCO requirement). Test method: 3-pole fall-of-potential (FOP) method per IEC 61557-5.

6. Earth Fault Loop Impedance (Ze / Zs)

Loop impedance testing is the most important live test for fault protection verification. It confirms that in a fault condition, enough fault current will flow to operate the protective device within the required disconnection time.

6a. Key Definitions

  • Ze — External loop impedance at the origin of the installation (measured before the main switch at DB)
  • Zs — Total fault loop impedance at the furthest point of each circuit (Ze + R1 + R2)
  • R1 — Resistance of the line conductor from DB to furthest point
  • R2 — Resistance of the CPC from furthest point back to MET

6d. Ze Measurement — LESCO Supply in Pakistan

LESCO LV overhead supply in Pakistan typically presents Ze = 0.10–0.45 Ω at the service head. Underground cables in commercial areas can achieve Ze = 0.05–0.15 Ω. Record Ze before the installation test certificate is issued — this is a mandatory field on LESCO connection forms.

7. RCD / RCCB / RCBO Trip-Time Testing

RCDs provide additional protection against electric shock on TT systems (mandatory in Pakistan for all socket-outlet circuits per recent LESCO regulations) and additional protection on TN systems where Zs is too high for MCB-only protection. Testing per IEC 61008-1 (RCCB) and IEC 61009-1 (RCBO).

7b. Additional RCD Tests

  • Half-rated test (IΔn/2): RCD must NOT trip within 2× its rated trip time (selectivity check for upstream/downstream coordination)
  • Push-button test: Monthly operational test; RCD should trip within 300 ms. Not a substitute for instrument test.
  • Ramp test (optional): Start from 0, ramp up current — records actual trip threshold IΔ. Should be 50–100% of rated IΔn.

8. PAT Testing (Portable Appliance Testing) in Pakistan

PAT testing is not legally mandated in Pakistan by a single statute, but is increasingly required by:

10. EI Inspection — What Pakistan Electricity Inspectors Check

The Electricity Inspector's site visit (required for permanent connection category B-2 and above at LESCO, and all HV connections) typically includes:

  1. IR test result sheet: Signed by licensed electrical supervisor (PEC Category C or B). Minimum 1 MΩ per circuit at 500 V DC.
  2. Earth electrode test result: Ra value from 3-pole FOP test. Signed + stamped by licensed engineer.
  3. Single-line diagram (SLD): Stamped by PEC-registered electrical engineer. Must match actual installation.
  4. Equipment test certificates: Type test certificates for main switchboard (IEC 61439-1 / IEC 61439-2), MV switchgear (IEC 62271), and transformer (IEC 60076). NEPRA-recognised testing labs only.
  5. Cable schedule: With sizes, routes, protective device ratings cross-referenced to SLD.
  6. Switchboard FAT report: For MDB/MCC manufactured locally — basic checks: busbar torque, IR, hi-pot if specified, trip settings.
  7. DISCO clearance form: For connections ≥200 kVA (LESCO requirement) — pre-inspection approval from LESCO Technical Services.

Always maintain a complete commissioning dossier: all test result sheets signed, dated, and stamped. LESCO and NEPRA can audit installations for up to 5 years post-connection. 12. Commissioning Documentation Checklist — Pakistan EI Certificate

Before presenting to the Electricity Inspector:

  • ☐ Form EI-3: Application for connection / completion certificate
  • ☐ IR test results sheet (all circuits, signed, dated)
  • ☐ Earth electrode test result (FOP method, Ra value)
  • ☐ Loop impedance test results (Ze at origin, Zs at each MCB furthest point)
  • ☐ RCD trip-time results (each RCD at IΔn and 5×IΔn)
  • ☐ As-built single-line diagram (SLD) — PEC-registered engineer stamp
  • ☐ Equipment type test certificates (MDB, transformer, switchgear)
  • ☐ LESCO/DISCO pre-inspection approval (≥200 kVA installations)
  • ☐ Licensed Electrical Contractor (LEC) certificate — valid PEC registration
  • ☐ Electrical supervisor qualification — PEC Category C (up to 100 kW) or Category B (above)
