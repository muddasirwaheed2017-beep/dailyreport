1. Why LED Flood Lights Now — WAPDA Tariff & Load-Shedding Context

Pakistan's C2 commercial/industrial electricity tariff reached PKR 38–52/kWh in 2026 (blended average PKR 43/kWh after FCA surcharges). Metal halide and high-pressure sodium (HPS) flood lights — the standard for outdoor industrial lighting through the 2010s — consume 250W–1,000W per fitting. At Pakistani tariff rates, a single 400W MH flood running 12 hours/day costs PKR 75,192/year in electricity alone.

LED flood lights at 100W–150W deliver equivalent or greater maintained illuminance. Replacing one 400W MH with one 100W LED saves PKR 56,394/year per fitting. A factory perimeter with 20 flood lights saves PKR 1.1 million/year — with payback under 4 months at current tariff levels.

Load-shedding adds a second dimension: smaller wattage LED loads run longer on UPS or generator backup. A 100W LED flood vs 400W MH means a generator running the factory perimeter security lighting needs only 25% the fuel cost per hour during grid outages.

2. Seven Pakistan-Specific Differentiators

Differentiator 1 — WAPDA Load-Shedding: LED Wattage = Generator Sizing

Generator running cost at PKR 280/litre diesel = PKR 168/kWh (6 kWh/litre at 1kVA). A 20-fixture perimeter with 400W MH = 8 kW generator load = PKR 1,344/hour. Same perimeter with 100W LED = 2 kW load = PKR 336/hour. LED slashes genset fuel cost 75% during load-shedding — the fastest payback argument in Pakistan's context.

Differentiator 2 — Pakistan 45–50°C Ambient: Driver Ta Derating is Mandatory

Most LED driver datasheets rate Ta=50°C as the absolute maximum case temperature. In Jacobabad, Dadu, and Multan where ambient reaches 48–52°C, driver derating of 15–20% output is mandatory. A 100W LED flood operating at 85W continuous in summer = 15% longer driver life. Always specify Ta=55°C rated driver (Meanwell LPF-90H, HLG-120H) for Punjab/Sindh outdoor installations.

Differentiator 3 — Monsoon Flood Zones: IP66 Minimum for Sindh/Punjab

Pakistan's monsoon season (July–September) brings standing water in most Sindh and southern Punjab outdoor areas. IP65 resists water jets but not prolonged immersion or high-pressure monsoon spray at low angles. IP66 is the minimum specification for any outdoor flood light in monsoon flood-risk areas. Gasketed die-cast aluminium housing with stainless steel fasteners is mandatory — zinc-coated steel corrodes in 2–3 monsoon seasons.

Differentiator 4 — Karachi Coastal Corrosion: IP66 + Marine-Grade Spec

Karachi's saline coastal atmosphere (DHS Zone 4 per IEC 60068-2-52) degrades standard aluminium housings in 18–24 months. Specification for Karachi and coastal Balochistan: IP66 minimum + anodised or powder-coated marine-grade aluminium + conformal-coated LED PCB + stainless steel 316 fasteners + silicone gaskets. Glass lens (not polycarbonate) resists UV yellowing in coastal UV index 9–11.

Differentiator 5 — Petrol Station / CNG Canopy: ATEX/IECEx Zone 2 Classification

Pakistan has 12,000+ CNG stations and 10,000+ petrol stations requiring hazardous area lighting. Zone 2 (IEC 60079-10-1) applies at forecourt canopies within 1m of dispenser. Standard IP66 LED flood lights are NOT rated for hazardous areas. Ex-rated LED floodlights (ATEX Zone 2 or IECEx certified) are required. Failure to use ATEX-rated fittings in CNG forecourts creates fire/explosion liability under OGRA regulations.

Differentiator 6 — Cricket / Sports Ground Floodlights: PCB Minimum 750 Lux

Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) venue standard for domestic cricket: minimum 750 lux (training), 1,500 lux (national broadcast). Sports-grade LED flood lights with narrow beam optics (10°–30°) and high CRI (≥80) are required. Generic low-angle flood lights with 120° beam spread waste 60% of output on sky glow — illegal under PCB lighting regulations and NEPA dark-sky guidance for major cities.

Differentiator 7 — PSQCA PS 4778:2022 Mandatory Certification

All LED luminaires sold in Pakistan must carry PSQCA certification under SRO 991(I)/2022. Market inspection squads in Lahore, Karachi, and Faisalabad confiscate non-certified product. For purchases ≥5 units, always request: (a) PSQCA certificate number, (b) batch test report from a PNAC-accredited lab, (c) CCC/CE/SAA as supplementary. Verify certificate validity at psqca.com.pk before bulk purchase.

4. Key Specifications Explained

4.1 Luminous Efficacy (lm/W)

Minimum acceptable for industrial Pakistan use: 130 lm/W. Budget SMD products at 80–100 lm/W are common in local markets but will not achieve equivalent illuminance claimed. Always request the independent IES/photometric test file, not the manufacturer's datasheet figure.

4.2 IP Rating

  • IP65 — dust-tight + water jet (household standard). Minimum for general outdoor use.
  • IP66 — dust-tight + powerful water jets. Recommended for all industrial outdoor use in Pakistan.
  • IP67 — dust-tight + temporary immersion (30 min, 1m). Use for recessed in-ground or flood-prone areas.
  • IP68 — continuous immersion. Swimming pools, water features, underground passages.

4.3 Driver Brand and Warranty

Driver is the most failure-prone component. Tier 1: Meanwell LPF/HLG series (5-year warranty, Ta=70°C). Tier 2: ERP, Sosen (3-year). Tier 3: generic Chinese OEM (often 1-year, not Ta rated). Specify Meanwell for any installation requiring 5+ year warranty. LED driver guide: LED Driver Price Pakistan 2026.

4.4 Colour Temperature (CCT)

  • 4000K–5000K (cool white): Recommended for security and perimeter (high visual acuity, CCTV camera sensitivity).
  • 3000K (warm white): Hospitality, hotel forecourts, retail exterior.
  • 5700K–6500K (daylight): Sports grounds, cricket pitches (matches broadcast camera white balance).

4.5 Beam Angle

  • 10°–30° — Narrow. Sports/stadium, long-throw perimeter (pole 15m+ height).
  • 45°–60° — Medium. Building façade, high-bay replacement.
  • 90°–120° — Wide. Parking lots, open area coverage at low mounting heights.
  • Asymmetric optics — Area lights for road/car park; projects light forward, minimises sky glow and glare.

4.6 Surge Protection (SPD built-in)

Pakistan WAPDA distribution network frequently suffers voltage spikes (2–4 kV transients during monsoon switching). All outdoor LED flood lights must have built-in SPD, minimum 4kV line-to-line. Verify this in the datasheet — many budget products claim "surge protection" but only to 1kV. For factory perimeter installations, also install an external IEC 61643 Type 2 SPD at the outdoor distribution board. See: SPD Surge Protection Device Price Pakistan 2026.

7. Solar Hybrid Flood Lights for Pakistan

Off-grid solar flood lights are ideal for: (a) remote agricultural/farm security where grid connection costs PKR 80,000–200,000, (b) rural road lighting under PSDP, (c) construction sites with temporary power needs.

7.1 All-in-One vs Split System

All-in-One (AIO): Solar panel + LED + battery + MPPT + PIR sensor integrated in one unit. Best for 50W–100W, simple installation, minimal vandalism risk in rural areas (one piece to steal vs multiple components). Weakness: when one component fails, entire unit must be replaced.
Split System: Separate pole-mount panel + ground/pole-mount battery box + LED flood. Better for 100W–200W, more repairable, battery replaceable independently. Recommended for commercial rural applications.

7.2 Battery Chemistry for Pakistan Climate

LiFePO4 (LFP): Recommended. Cycle life 2,000–4,000 cycles (5–10 years). Operates to 60°C ambient (critical for summer). PKR premium 40–60% over VRLA but 3× the life in Pakistan conditions.
VRLA/AGM: Lower cost, but 45°C ambient cuts cycle life to 200–300 cycles (18 months in Punjab summer). Avoid for solar flood applications where battery is exposed to ambient temperature.

See also: Solar Battery Storage System Price Pakistan 2026.

7.3 Automatic Control — Photocell vs Timer vs PIR

Photocell/LDR: Best for continuous dusk-to-dawn operation. Self-adjusts for Pakistan latitude (sunset 18:30–21:00 summer, 17:30–18:30 winter). See: Photocell LDR Switch Price Pakistan 2026.
PIR Sensor: Motion-triggered. Saves 60–70% battery in low-traffic perimeter areas. Set to 50% dim when no motion, 100% on motion.
Timer: Useful where motion patterns are known (factory shift end/start at fixed times). Program for WAPDA peak hours (17:00–22:00) to align with tariff structure.

9. Installation Best Practices

9.1 Pole Height and Spacing

  • Factory perimeter: 8m–12m pole height, 20m–30m spacing for 150W–200W fittings at 50 lux target.
  • Parking lot: 6m–8m pole, 15m–20m spacing for 100W fittings.
  • Cricket/sports: 15m–25m floodlight mast, specialized sports lighting design required.

9.2 Earthing and Surge Protection

All outdoor lighting poles must be earthed to a rod electrode (≤10 Ω per IEC 60364-5-54). Install Type 2 SPD at outdoor distribution board feeding flood lights. Pakistan WAPDA switching transients regularly exceed 2kV — unprotected drivers fail within 1–2 monsoon seasons. See: SPD Surge Protection Device Price Pakistan 2026.

9.3 Photocell Placement

Mount photocell facing north (to avoid direct sunlight false triggering) at pole top, separate from the flood light (heat from flood light creates false dawn detection if co-mounted). Use a 10-second delay off timer to prevent relay chatter at dusk. See: Photocell LDR Switch Pakistan Guide.

9.4 Cable Selection for Outdoor Runs

Outdoor underground cable: 1.5 mm² XLPE armoured (≤30m runs), 2.5 mm² for ≤60m, 4 mm² for ≤100m at 150W per fitting. Run in GI conduit where exposed above grade. Never use PVC sheathed cable in direct soil burial in Pakistan — UV degradation + rodent damage within 3–5 years. Armoured cable with PVC outer sheath (BS 5467 / equivalent) in direct burial is the standard.

9.5 Power Factor Correction

LED drivers above 75W typically have PF ≥ 0.95 built-in (Meanwell HLG series). Economy grade drivers often have PF 0.7–0.85. For WAPDA commercial connections ≥50kW, low PF triggers NEPRA penalty. Verify driver PF spec; install panel-level capacitor bank if aggregate PF < 0.85. See: APFC Automatic Power Factor Correction Panel Pakistan 2026.

10. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of LED flood light in Pakistan 2026?

LED flood light prices in Pakistan 2026 range from PKR 1,800–3,500 for 50W economy grade, PKR 4,500–8,000 for 100W commercial grade, PKR 9,000–16,000 for 200W industrial IP66 grade, PKR 18,000–32,000 for 300W heavy-duty, and PKR 35,000–65,000 for 500W stadium/industrial-perimeter grade.

What IP rating is needed for outdoor flood lights in Pakistan?

IP65 is the minimum for outdoor use. For monsoon flood zones in Sindh/Punjab, IP66 is mandatory. Karachi coastal installations require IP66 + marine-grade aluminium + conformal-coated LED PCB.

How much does a 100W LED flood light save on WAPDA bill?

Replacing a 400W metal halide with a 100W LED saves 300W per fitting. At PKR 43/kWh, 12 hours/day: annual saving = PKR 56,394 per fitting. Payback on a PKR 8,000 LED unit is under 2 months at current tariff.

What wattage LED flood replaces 400W metal halide?

A 100W–150W LED COB flood (≥130 lm/W) replaces a 400W MH. Pakistan summer consideration: MH output degrades 40% at 48°C ambient by 4,000 hours — the effective LED:MH replacement ratio is 1:4 at end-of-life.

Can I use LED flood lights with solar in Pakistan?

Yes. Solar hybrid flood lights (50W–200W) with integrated MPPT + LiFePO4 battery work entirely off-grid. Above 200W, separate solar panel + grid-tied inverter + LED flood is more economical. LiFePO4 chemistry is mandatory for Pakistan's 45°C+ summer ambient.

What is the PSQCA requirement for LED flood lights?

PSQCA PS 4778:2022 certification is mandatory under SRO 991(I)/2022. Request the certificate number and verify at psqca.com.pk for any purchase ≥5 fittings.

How many flood lights for a parking area?

Rule of thumb: one 150W LED flood (beam 60°×120°) covers approximately 20m × 15m at 8m pole height achieving 50 lux. For a 50-car lot (30m × 40m): 6 × 150W fittings on 8m poles. Use DIALux for precise design.

Which brands are available in Pakistan?

Philips, Osram, Cree (premium); local assembled with Meanwell driver (best value for industrial). CNC Electric Pakistan stocks industrial-grade IP66 flood lights with 5-year warranties.
